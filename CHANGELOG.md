# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-27)

First public release.

- Detailed prompts across 11 sales jobs, each with a detailed version, a quick version, follow-ups and tips.
- 10 playbooks that chain prompts into end-to-end workflows.
- Role guides for 8 sales roles.
- Interactive library website, CSV export, and PDF guides (quick-start, full catalogue, playbooks, role cheat sheets).
- Every Draup for Sales prompt was tested against live Draup data on public companies and refined over three review rounds.
