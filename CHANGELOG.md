# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-27)

- New [Connect Draup to your AI](guides/connect.md) guide: add Draup for Sales from Claude's directory or as a ChatGPT plugin, or connect any MCP client (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, custom agents) using Draup's MCP documentation.
- Redesigned landing page with one-click "Add to Claude" and "Add to ChatGPT" links, a how-it-works diagram, and clearer tables.
- Navigation bar on every prompt, role, category and playbook page; quick versions folded under each detailed prompt.
- Prompts now work in Claude or ChatGPT ("Use Draup for Sales for every fact"); the start-here prompt is renamed "Tell your AI about you".
- Site: connect buttons, help links; PDFs refreshed.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-27)

First public release.

- Detailed prompts across 11 sales jobs, each with a detailed version, a quick version, follow-ups and tips.
- 10 playbooks that chain prompts into end-to-end workflows.
- Role guides for 8 sales roles.
- Interactive library website, CSV export, and PDF guides (quick-start, full catalogue, playbooks, role cheat sheets).
- Every Draup for Sales prompt was tested against live Draup data on public companies and refined over three review rounds.
