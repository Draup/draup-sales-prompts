What this is

A library of ready-made prompts for Claude and ChatGPT. With Draup for Sales switched on, your assistant pulls live Draup data and turns it into account briefs, buyer maps, target lists, signal alerts and outreach drafts. You fill in a few blanks, and it does the research.

Good for

  • Preparing for meetings in minutes
  • Finding the right buyers and a warm way in
  • Knowing why now before you reach out
  • Building and sizing target lists
  • Mapping incumbent vendors and competitors
  • Account plans, risk checks and QBR prep

What you need

  • Claude (claude.ai, desktop or mobile) or ChatGPT
  • A Draup account with Draup for Sales access
  • About two minutes to connect the two

Set up in 3 steps

  1. Connect Draup. Claude: open claude.ai/directory/draup-for-sales (or Settings → Connectors → Browse connectors), click Connect and sign in with your Draup account. ChatGPT: open Plugins, search “Draup for Sales”, add it and sign in. Other MCP clients: see the Connect guide on GitHub. It is read-only.
  2. Tell your AI about you. Paste the start-here prompt (next page) into a new chat, fill in your details and send it. For a permanent setup, paste it into a Claude or ChatGPT Project’s instructions.
  3. Run a prompt. Pick one from this guide or the catalogue, replace the [bracketed] parts and send. Then use the follow-ups to go deeper in the same chat.

Tips for great answers

Do thisWhy
Use the company website (siemens.com) rather than the nameMatches the right company first time, not a subsidiary or a look-alike
Name a business function or workload ("IT", "Supply Chain", "AI & Data Science")Big companies have thousands of records; narrowing gives sharper answers
Ask for top N, a time window and datesKeeps answers short and shows you how fresh each fact is
Use the detailed prompt for anything you will shareIt carries the guardrails: dated facts, "Not in Draup" instead of guesses
Keep going in the same chat with follow-upsClaude reuses what it already found
Save your solution offering in your Draup profileUnlocks Draup opportunities tailored to what you sell

Reading the answers

  • Financials are annual, in the company's reporting currency and fiscal year. Ask for USD when comparing.
  • Outsourced headcount is a Draup estimate, not a reported figure.
  • Executive and news data is a starting point. Check names and titles before you send anything.
  • "Not in Draup" means no data was found, which is not the same as "doesn't exist".
