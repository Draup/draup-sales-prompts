# Prompts for Services, SI & Staffing Seller

Map outsourcing incumbents, share of wallet, GCCs and displacement wedges.

46 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Is their budget growing or shrinking?](../prompts/01-account-research/budget-posture.md) | A verdict on whether the account's budget is growing, holding or shrinking, the IT spend split, and the deal size and pitch that fit. |
| [Which priority to anchor your pitch on](../prompts/01-account-research/priorities-by-function.md) | Where the account's priorities cluster, how each fits what you sell, and the single priority to lead with, with its likely owner and linked vendors. |
| [Which region should own this account](../prompts/01-account-research/buying-center.md) | Which region and metros hold the revenue, people and decision makers for your function, which region should own the pursuit, and where to hold the first meeting. |
| [Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create](../prompts/01-account-research/acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. |
| [Account SWOT, outside-in](../prompts/01-account-research/swot.md) | A SWOT of the account built only from dated Draup evidence, ending with where your offer fits and who to take it to. |

## Signals and timing

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Are they hiring more than usual?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/hiring-spike.md) | A plain-English verdict on whether job postings are running above or below normal, which workloads drive it, and whether it's seasonal. |
| [What their job ads say about their stack](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/practice-shift.md) | The software tools in the account's job ads, matched against its installed stack and sorted into New, Building, Core and Coasting, so you see where people and budget are moving before it hits the press. |
| [Priorities with no hiring behind them](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/priorities-vs-hiring.md) | Which stated priorities have hiring behind them and which don't: the gaps where the account may need to buy rather than build. |
| [Who in my market just [did X]?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/news-question-templates.md) | A clean, dated table of the companies in your industry and country that just did one thing you choose: M&A, layoffs, leadership changes, expansion or new centres, funding, or partnerships. |
| [Who just picked a vendor](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vendor-selection-news.md) | A dated list of companies in your industry that just selected, deployed or replaced a vendor in your category: competitor wins to watch, peer proof, and the implementation partners involved. |

## Outsourcing and services deals

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|

| [A competitor's footprint at an account](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-at-account.md) | How deep a rival services firm is at the account, how it delivers, and the workloads where it is weakest: your wedge. |
| [Competitor's customers in my vertical](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-customers.md) | A ranked list of a rival's customers in your vertical and country, with how entrenched it is at each: your displacement targets. |

| [Map my offering to buyers](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/offering-to-buyers.md) | Your offering mapped to Draup workloads you approve, then the 20 companies in your territory that outsource them most, with their incumbents and where the provider mix is fragmented. |
| [Profile a services firm (rival or partner)](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-profile.md) | A services firm's size, top customers, workloads and delivery hubs in your segment, ending in how to out-position it (rival) or where to team with it (partner). |
| [Who to partner with vs replace, by priority](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/partner-map.md) | For each priority in one function, the providers and tech vendors linked to it, and whether to partner, displace or walk into an open door. |
| [Is work coming back in-house? (re-insourcing risk)](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/reinsourcing-risk.md) | For each outsourced workload, whether the account is building the same skills in-house, plus fast-growing in-house teams outside its outsourced list: cost takeout, capability gap or re-insourcing risk. |
| [Global capability centres (GCCs) and the services play](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/gcc-opportunity.md) | The account's global capability centres (company-owned offshore or captive sites): where, which functions, how fast they hire, likely new sites, and whether build-operate-transfer (BOT), centre-of-excellence (CoE) expansion or managed services fits. |
| [Vendor mix vs the vertical](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/vendor-mix-benchmark.md) | Where the account outsources more or less than its industry, and which leading industry providers it doesn't use yet. |

## Playbooks for you

- [**Win accounts from a services competitor**](../playbooks/playbook-displacement-campaign.md): Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message.
- [**Global capability centre (GCC) & outsourcing pursuit**](../playbooks/playbook-gcc-services-pursuit.md): Size an account's outsourcing and captive-centre (GCC) footprint, read its sourcing intent, check the main incumbent, and build a pursuit brief for the executive sponsor.
- [**Partner co-sell**](../playbooks/playbook-partner-cosell.md): Find where partners are already embedded at a target account, pick the right one to co-sell with, and open jointly.
