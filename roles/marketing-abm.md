# Prompts for Marketing & ABM

Pick target accounts and write messages tied to what each account is doing.

61 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Start with these 5

| # | Prompt | What you get | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [ABM openers for 10 accounts at once](../prompts/16-content-and-enablement/abm-messages.md) | A table of short openers for up to 10 accounts per pass, each written to your persona in their language and tied to one stated priority and one dated signal from the last 60 days, graded Strong, OK or Weak, with sources and an optional joint partner note. | 6–10 min |
| 2 | [Build an industry point of view](../prompts/14-industry-intelligence/industry-pov.md) | The 5 industry priorities that matter most for what you sell, each with dated company proof and a likely owner, turned into a 5-slide narrative, a one-line POV and a named first contact. | 2–4 min |
| 3 | [Size your ideal-customer list](../prompts/06-prospecting-and-lists/icp-list.md) | The true count of companies matching every filter, distributors set aside, and the 5 accounts to start with, each with who to call and a dated why-now. | 4–6 min |
| 4 | [3-email sequence for one industry persona](../prompts/16-content-and-enablement/email-sequence.md) | A ready-to-load 3-email sequence for one persona in one industry, each email built on a priority in the persona's function and a dated move by a real company, plus the first account to load with a named contact. | 2–4 min |
| 5 | [What a rival did this quarter](../prompts/09-competitive-intelligence/recent-moves.md) | The few moves a competitor made in the last 90 days that change your deals (customer wins, partnerships, launches, deals and leadership), the accounts to defend or attack, and the named person to call at the top one. | 2–4 min |

> **Before you trust a number**
> - Numbers: IT spend is a Draup estimate, often modelled from revenue, so use it for scale, not as a signal. Industry totals are global. Financials are in the company's reporting currency and fiscal year.
> - Freshness: news and hiring update continuously; leadership moves can lag by about three months, and brand-new executives may not appear yet.
> - People: some senior leaders have no function tag, so the prompts widen to C-level titles when needed. Draup can't search people by name.
> - Outsourcing engagements are Draup-derived records of work between a company and its providers, not published contracts.
> - Every drafted email ends with a "Check before sending" line listing any single-source or conflicting facts. Competitor tools are rated Red, Amber or Green by how entrenched they are.
> - Give the website the company actually uses (some parents use domains like home.barclays), and check names, titles and numbers before anything goes to a customer.

## Playbooks for you

- [**Take accounts from a rival services firm**](../playbooks/playbook-displacement-campaign.md): Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message.
- [**Territory and list building: ICP to territories**](../playbooks/playbook-territory-list.md): Turn what your best customers have in common into a sized list, tier it on fit and timing for what you sell, carve it into balanced territories, and send the first touches to the top tier.
- [**Break into a new industry**](../playbooks/playbook-new-industry.md): Learn a new industry fast, brief the team on its language and buyers, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view.
- [**Build a quarter's pipeline for one solution**](../playbooks/playbook-solution-play.md): Go from one solution area (for example cloud migration, data, ERP, security or HR tech) to a scored target list, qualified accounts with a named buyer, the incumbents to work around, and timed first touches that cite each account's own evidence.
- [**Take accounts from a rival software vendor**](../playbooks/playbook-competitive-takeout.md): Pick a rival, understand where it's heading, find the accounts where its product is weakest, and open those accounts with a timely, evidence-based message.
- [**Sales leader plan: market to coverage**](../playbooks/playbook-plan-next-quarter-territory.md): As a sales leader, size the market for what you sell, pick where to add the next rep, tier the accounts, carve balanced territories, set coverage and next quarter's targets, then run the plan week to week.
- [**Launch a co-sell programme with a partner**](../playbooks/playbook-partner-program-launch.md): Choose the right services partner for an industry, check it's heading the right way, build the joint pipeline, plan the first account together and arm the partner's sellers.
- [**Run an ABM campaign for 10–20 accounts**](../playbooks/playbook-abm-campaign.md): Pick 10–20 target accounts, tier them, write each one a personalised opener from its own priorities and signals, multithread the top accounts, nurture the rest and refresh the list every week.
