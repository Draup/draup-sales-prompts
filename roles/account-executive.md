# Prompts for Account Executive

Win new logos: research, buyers, timing and a sharp first meeting.

82 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** run [Tell Claude about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [One-page account brief](../prompts/01-account-research/account-brief.md) | A one-page brief on size, money, priorities, recent deals and any competitor in the stack, ending with who to call and why now. |
| [10-minute pre-call snapshot](../prompts/01-account-research/precall-snapshot.md) | Five short bullets you can read in the lobby: who they are, what just happened, where hiring is rising, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. |
| [Is their budget growing or shrinking?](../prompts/01-account-research/budget-posture.md) | A verdict on whether the account's budget is growing, holding or shrinking, the IT spend split, and the deal size and pitch that fit. |
| [Which priority to anchor your pitch on](../prompts/01-account-research/priorities-by-function.md) | Where the account's priorities cluster, how each fits what you sell, and the single priority to lead with, with its likely owner and linked vendors. |
| [Their leaders' own words on [Topic]](../prompts/01-account-research/proof-points.md) | Up to six verbatim quotes from leadership on your topic, with speaker, fiscal quarter and call date, or the annual-filing sections to read when there's no call. |
| [Numbers from the latest call](../prompts/01-account-research/numbers-from-the-call.md) | Every number management stated on the latest earnings call (guidance, targets, KPIs, investment plans) in one table, with the three that matter for your deal and the question each opens. |
| [What changed between two calls](../prompts/01-account-research/call-over-call.md) | Themes that are new, stronger, weaker or dropped between the last two earnings calls, each with a quote, plus a 2-line talk track for your next QBR. |
| [Which region should own this account](../prompts/01-account-research/buying-center.md) | Which region and metros hold the revenue, people and decision makers for your function, which region should own the pursuit, and where to hold the first meeting. |
| [Fastest-growing business units](../prompts/01-account-research/business-units.md) | Business units ranked by revenue growth, with the two worth targeting, the priority behind each and the unit leader's title. |
| [Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create](../prompts/01-account-research/acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. |
| [Private company funding](../prompts/01-account-research/private-funding.md) | How much a private company has raised, its last round and valuation, equity history, whether it is in growth mode, and who signs. |
| [Account SWOT, outside-in](../prompts/01-account-research/swot.md) | A SWOT of the account built only from dated Draup evidence, ending with where your offer fits and who to take it to. |

| [Is a competitor installed?](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/competitor-installed.md) | A clear verdict on whether a rival's products are live anywhere in the account's stack, how central they are, who else is in the room and which stated priorities could justify a switch. |
| [What they adopted this year](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/recent-adoptions.md) | The tools first detected at the account in the last 12 months, each labelled as competing with, complementing or unrelated to what you sell, with any competitor product called out first. |

## Signals and timing

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [What's worth acting on now](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/whats-actionable.md) | Up to 5 real triggers from the last 90 days, each tied to a stated priority, a likely owner and a suggested action, with the noise stripped out. |
| [Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md) | For one account or up to 5 per pass: the freshest relevant signal, the priority it supports, and a short, sourced opener for your persona that ends in one low-friction ask. |
| [Are they hiring more than usual?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/hiring-spike.md) | A plain-English verdict on whether job postings are running above or below normal, which workloads drive it, and whether it's seasonal. |
| [Are they really investing in X?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/investing-in-x.md) | A Yes / Early / No verdict on whether hiring, headcount and stated priorities back up a technology theme, with proof postings and talking points. |
| [What their job ads say about their stack](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/practice-shift.md) | The software tools in the account's job ads, matched against its installed stack and sorted into New, Building, Core and Coasting, so you see where people and budget are moving before it hits the press. |
| [Priorities with no hiring behind them](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/priorities-vs-hiring.md) | Which stated priorities have hiring behind them and which don't: the gaps where the account may need to buy rather than build. |
| [Who in my market just [did X]?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/news-question-templates.md) | A clean, dated table of the companies in your industry and country that just did one thing you choose: M&A, layoffs, leadership changes, expansion or new centres, funding, or partnerships. |
| [Who just picked a vendor](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vendor-selection-news.md) | A dated list of companies in your industry that just selected, deployed or replaced a vendor in your category: competitor wins to watch, peer proof, and the implementation partners involved. |
| [What changed since [Date]](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/change-log.md) | A dated log of everything material that changed at an account since a date you choose, ranked by impact on your deal. |
| [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md) | One table of the week's real triggers across up to 10 accounts, each with a so-what and an action, plus a clean list of accounts with nothing new. |
| [Weekly industry signal digest](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vertical-digest.md) | A one-screen digest of the week's material moves in one industry, grouped by theme, with the accounts worth touching. |

## Playbooks for you

- [First meeting in 30 minutes](../playbooks/playbook-first-meeting.md): Walk into a first meeting knowing the account, the people in the room and the questions that will earn a second meeting.
- [Win accounts from a services competitor](../playbooks/playbook-displacement-campaign.md): Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message.
- [Weekly signal routine](../playbooks/playbook-weekly-signals.md): Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.
- [Existing customer: account plan → risk → QBR](../playbooks/playbook-account-plan-qbr.md): Build or refresh the plan for a key customer, turn it into concrete expansion plays, check it for risk, and walk into the QBR with a point of view and clear asks.
- [Partner co-sell](../playbooks/playbook-partner-cosell.md): Find where partners are already embedded at a target account, pick the right one to co-sell with, and open jointly.
- [Territory and list building](../playbooks/playbook-territory-list.md): Turn an ICP into a sized, prioritised and tiered account list your team can work this quarter.
- [Enter a new industry](../playbooks/playbook-new-industry.md): Learn a new industry fast, see who wins there, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view.
- [Expand an existing customer](../playbooks/playbook-expand-customer.md): Find where an existing customer is growing, pick the best way in for your next deal, map the people, and open with a timely message.
- [Renewal defence](../playbooks/playbook-renewal-defence.md): Spot what has changed at a customer ahead of renewal, rate the risk, rebuild relationships with new leaders, and walk into the review with proof of value.
