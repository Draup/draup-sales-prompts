# Prompts for Presales & Solutions

Understand architecture, priorities and incumbents before the technical conversation.

34 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [One-page account brief](../prompts/01-account-research/account-brief.md) | A one-page brief on size, money, priorities, recent deals and any competitor in the stack, ending with who to call and why now. |
| [10-minute pre-call snapshot](../prompts/01-account-research/precall-snapshot.md) | Five short bullets you can read in the lobby: who they are, what just happened, where hiring is rising, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. |
| [Which priority to anchor your pitch on](../prompts/01-account-research/priorities-by-function.md) | Where the account's priorities cluster, how each fits what you sell, and the single priority to lead with, with its likely owner and linked vendors. |
| [Their leaders' own words on [Topic]](../prompts/01-account-research/proof-points.md) | Up to six verbatim quotes from leadership on your topic, with speaker, fiscal quarter and call date, or the annual-filing sections to read when there's no call. |
| [Numbers from the latest call](../prompts/01-account-research/numbers-from-the-call.md) | Every number management stated on the latest earnings call (guidance, targets, KPIs, investment plans) in one table, with the three that matter for your deal and the question each opens. |
| [What changed between two calls](../prompts/01-account-research/call-over-call.md) | Themes that are new, stronger, weaker or dropped between the last two earnings calls, each with a quote, plus a 2-line talk track for your next QBR. |
| [Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create](../prompts/01-account-research/acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. |

## Buyers and stakeholders

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Map the buying committee](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/buying-committee.md) | A ranked list of the people likely to buy, influence or block your deal in one function or workload, with their role in the decision, an entry point and the sponsor to reach later. |
| [Who uses [Technology] there](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/personas-by-tool.md) | Which roles at the account use a given tool, grouped into three buyer personas with the other tools they use and the message for each. |
| [Hypothesis-led discovery questions](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/discovery-questions.md) | An opening insight and eight discovery questions, each built on a dated fact about the account, with the hypothesis behind it and what a good answer tells you. |
| [Pre-fill MEDDPICC from outside-in data](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/meddpicc-prefill.md) | A MEDDPICC sheet pre-filled with what Draup can show (metrics, buyer candidates, pain, competition) and clear 'must discover' flags for the rest. |

## Tech stack and displacement

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Their stack in one category](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/stack-category.md) | What they run in one category, ranked by how actively each tool is used, with stale tools set aside, unfamiliar products explained and an optional vendor ranking with a consolidation read. |
| [Is a competitor installed?](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/competitor-installed.md) | A clear verdict on whether a rival's products are live anywhere in the account's stack, how central they are, who else is in the room and which stated priorities could justify a switch. |
| [What they adopted this year](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/recent-adoptions.md) | The tools first detected at the account in the last 12 months, each labelled as competing with, complementing or unrelated to what you sell, with any competitor product called out first. |
| [Fading stack: replacement windows](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/fading-stack.md) | The tools that have gone quiet in one area, what seems to be taking their place, and the 3 best-timed replacement pitches. |
| [Size a vendor's install base](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/vendor-install-base.md) | What a vendor sells and how many companies use each product, so you can size an attach, co-sell or competitive-replacement motion. |
| [Who's already working each priority](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/priority-stack-incumbent-map.md) | For each stated priority in one function: the tools linked to it, the service providers working it, and whether to displace, co-sell or go greenfield, judged for a software or a services seller. |

## Signals and timing

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Are they really investing in X?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/investing-in-x.md) | A Yes / Early / No verdict on whether hiring, headcount and stated priorities back up a technology theme, with proof postings and talking points. |
| [What their job ads say about their stack](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/practice-shift.md) | The software tools in the account's job ads, matched against its installed stack and sorted into New, Building, Core and Coasting, so you see where people and budget are moving before it hits the press. |
| [Priorities with no hiring behind them](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/priorities-vs-hiring.md) | Which stated priorities have hiring behind them and which don't: the gaps where the account may need to buy rather than build. |

## Account planning and expansion

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|

## Content and enablement

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Battlecard: rival services firm](../prompts/09-content-and-enablement/battlecard-services.md) | A one-page internal battlecard on a rival services firm: where it delivers, what it wins, recent moves, how to beat it, trap questions and the objection to expect. |
| [Battlecard: rival software vendor](../prompts/09-content-and-enablement/battlecard-product.md) | A one-page internal battlecard on a rival software vendor: its most-used products, the largest companies running them, recent moves, how to beat it and the objection to expect. |
| ["How does Draup know this?"](../prompts/09-content-and-enablement/how-draup-knows.md) | A plain-English, shareable answer on how Draup builds a number (IT spend, outsourcing size, talent flow and so on), with the help-centre link. |

## Talent cost and location

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Offshore cost case (base-pay gap)](../prompts/10-talent-cost-and-location/offshoring-cost-case.md) | A per-FTE and team-level base-pay gap for moving a role offshore, with the talent supply and hiring difficulty to show the team can be staffed. |
| [Where to put a delivery team](../prompts/10-talent-cost-and-location/delivery-location.md) | A ranked shortlist of metros in one country for a delivery team, scored on talent supply, cost, hiring difficulty and location risk with editable weights, plus the client's existing footprint. |
| [Pay benchmark and rate-card check](../prompts/10-talent-cost-and-location/pay-benchmark.md) | Market pay for a role by employer, including named competitors, the markup a proposed or quoted rate implies, and the rate you'd need at your target markup. |
