# Prompts for Account & Customer Success Manager

Grow and protect existing accounts: risk checks, expansion, QBRs.

33 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** run [Tell Claude about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [One-page account brief](../prompts/01-account-research/account-brief.md) | A one-page brief on size, money, priorities, recent deals and any competitor in the stack, ending with who to call and why now. |
| [10-minute pre-call snapshot](../prompts/01-account-research/precall-snapshot.md) | Five short bullets you can read in the lobby: who they are, what just happened, where hiring is rising, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. |
| [Is their budget growing or shrinking?](../prompts/01-account-research/budget-posture.md) | A verdict on whether the account's budget is growing, holding or shrinking, the IT spend split, and the deal size and pitch that fit. |
| [Which priority to anchor your pitch on](../prompts/01-account-research/priorities-by-function.md) | Where the account's priorities cluster, how each fits what you sell, and the single priority to lead with, with its likely owner and linked vendors. |
| [Their leaders' own words on [Topic]](../prompts/01-account-research/proof-points.md) | Up to six verbatim quotes from leadership on your topic, with speaker, fiscal quarter and call date, or the annual-filing sections to read when there's no call. |
| [Numbers from the latest call](../prompts/01-account-research/numbers-from-the-call.md) | Every number management stated on the latest earnings call (guidance, targets, KPIs, investment plans) in one table, with the three that matter for your deal and the question each opens. |
| [What changed between two calls](../prompts/01-account-research/call-over-call.md) | Themes that are new, stronger, weaker or dropped between the last two earnings calls, each with a quote, plus a 2-line talk track for your next QBR. |
| [Which region should own this account](../prompts/01-account-research/buying-center.md) | Which region and metros hold the revenue, people and decision makers for your function, which region should own the pursuit, and where to hold the first meeting. |
| [Fastest-growing business units](../prompts/01-account-research/business-units.md) | Business units ranked by revenue growth, with the two worth targeting, the priority behind each and the unit leader's title. |
| [Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create](../prompts/01-account-research/acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. |
| [Account SWOT, outside-in](../prompts/01-account-research/swot.md) | A SWOT of the account built only from dated Draup evidence, ending with where your offer fits and who to take it to. |

## Buyers and stakeholders

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Map the buying committee](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/buying-committee.md) | A ranked list of the people likely to buy, influence or block your deal in one function or workload, with their role in the decision, an entry point and the sponsor to reach later. |
| [New leaders in the last 6 months](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/new-leaders-warm-paths.md) | Leaders who joined or were promoted in the last 6 months in your area, where they came from, and which ones arrive from your customers. |
| [Warm paths among current leaders](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/warm-paths-current-leaders.md) | Senior leaders at the account who used to work at your customers, partners or your own company, so you can ask for a warm intro. |
| [Executive profile and openers](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/executive-profile.md) | A one-screen profile of a named executive and two openers tied to the account's current priorities. |
| [Hypothesis-led discovery questions](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/discovery-questions.md) | An opening insight and eight discovery questions, each built on a dated fact about the account, with the hypothesis behind it and what a good answer tells you. |
| [Pre-fill MEDDPICC from outside-in data](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/meddpicc-prefill.md) | A MEDDPICC sheet pre-filled with what Draup can show (metrics, buyer candidates, pain, competition) and clear 'must discover' flags for the rest. |

## Account planning and expansion

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Draft an account plan](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/account-plan.md) | An account plan in eight parts: snapshot, their strategy in their own words, buying committee, incumbents, ranked expansion plays, risks and a 90-day plan, plus an optional short SWOT. |
| [Entry points and whitespace](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/entry-points.md) | The five best ways into the account for what you sell, each with dated evidence, incumbents and the likely owner. |
| [Where they're building teams](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/expansion-footprint.md) | The metros where the account is adding people in one function, with GCCs and likely new sites called out and net hiring over a stated window. |
| [Renewal risk check (RAG)](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/risk-check.md) | A Red/Amber/Green risk table for one customer, including your sponsor's status, with dated evidence, an overall rating and two actions. |
| [Talent rivals: who they lose people to](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/talent-competitors.md) | The companies an account loses people to and hires from, with net flow: the employers it competes with for skills, and credible peers to benchmark against. |
| [Customer alumni on the move](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/follow-champions.md) | Senior people who recently left one of your customers, where they went, and which new employers are on your target list, with any champions you name flagged first. |
| [QBR prep](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/qbr-prep.md) | What changed at the customer during the review period, set against the outcomes you delivered, with three talking points and three asks. |
| [Tier my accounts by propensity to buy](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/propensity-tiering.md) | Up to 25 accounts scored 0–100 on an editable Fit (0–50) and Timing (0–50) rubric, tiered A, B or C and placed on a fit-versus-timing grid, with the driver behind each score. |

## Playbooks for you

- [Weekly signal routine](../playbooks/playbook-weekly-signals.md): Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.
- [Existing customer: account plan → risk → QBR](../playbooks/playbook-account-plan-qbr.md): Build or refresh the plan for a key customer, turn it into concrete expansion plays, check it for risk, and walk into the QBR with a point of view and clear asks.
- [Expand an existing customer](../playbooks/playbook-expand-customer.md): Find where an existing customer is growing, pick the best way in for your next deal, map the people, and open with a timely message.
- [Renewal defence](../playbooks/playbook-renewal-defence.md): Spot what has changed at a customer ahead of renewal, rate the risk, rebuild relationships with new leaders, and walk into the review with proof of value.
