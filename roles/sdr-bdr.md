# Prompts for SDR / BDR

Find who to contact and why now, then write outreach that references real facts.

66 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Start with these 5

| # | Prompt | What you get | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md) | One phone-sized table of the week's real triggers across up to 10 accounts, including any CEO or leadership change that takes effect in the window, plus the nearest item just outside the window for every quiet account. | 4–6 min |
| 2 | [What's worth acting on now](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/whats-actionable.md) | An Act, Watch or Skip verdict and up to 5 real triggers from the last 90 days, kept or dropped with what you sell in mind (incidents and regulatory inquiries count if they bear on your offer), each with a named owner and a source count. | 2–4 min |
| 3 | [Signal-led emails for up to 10 accounts](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md) | For one account or up to 10 per pass: the most relevant recent signal (preferring your territory), the priority it supports, a fact check, and a short opener written in your persona's language that ends in one easy ask. | 4–6 min |
| 4 | [Email a newly appointed executive](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/new-executive.md) | The newest senior appointments in your area at one account, checked against a second source, the stated priority that fits the new remit, and a 90-word first-90-days email plus a short follow-up. | 2–4 min |
| 5 | [Size your ideal-customer list](../prompts/06-prospecting-and-lists/icp-list.md) | The true count of companies matching every filter, distributors set aside, and the 5 accounts to start with, each with who to call and a dated why-now. | 4–6 min |

> **Before you trust a number**
> - Numbers: IT spend is a Draup estimate, often modelled from revenue, so use it for scale, not as a signal. Industry totals are global. Financials are in the company's reporting currency and fiscal year.
> - Freshness: news and hiring update continuously; leadership moves can lag by about three months, and brand-new executives may not appear yet.
> - People: some senior leaders have no function tag, so the prompts widen to C-level titles when needed. Draup can't search people by name.
> - Outsourcing engagements are Draup-derived records of work between a company and its providers, not published contracts.
> - Every drafted email ends with a "Check before sending" line listing any single-source or conflicting facts. Competitor tools are rated Red, Amber or Green by how entrenched they are.
> - Give the website the company actually uses (some parents use domains like home.barclays), and check names, titles and numbers before anything goes to a customer.

## Playbooks for you

- [**Be ready for a first meeting**](../playbooks/playbook-first-meeting.md): Walk into a first meeting knowing the account, the people in the room and the questions that will earn a second meeting.
- [**Take accounts from a rival services firm**](../playbooks/playbook-displacement-campaign.md): Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message.
- [**Monday routine: who to contact this week**](../playbooks/playbook-weekly-signals.md): Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.
- [**Territory and list building: ICP to territories**](../playbooks/playbook-territory-list.md): Turn what your best customers have in common into a sized list, tier it on fit and timing for what you sell, carve it into balanced territories, and send the first touches to the top tier.
- [**Break into a new industry**](../playbooks/playbook-new-industry.md): Learn a new industry fast, brief the team on its language and buyers, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view.
- [**Build a quarter's pipeline for one solution**](../playbooks/playbook-solution-play.md): Go from one solution area (for example cloud migration, data, ERP, security or HR tech) to a scored target list, qualified accounts with a named buyer, the incumbents to work around, and timed first touches that cite each account's own evidence.
- [**A week of trigger-based outreach**](../playbooks/playbook-trigger-outreach-week.md): Turn one week of real account triggers into timely, multi-channel outreach: every message tied to a dated public fact, routed to the right person, with a call and voicemail behind each email and a clean re-open for stalled deals.
- [**Take accounts from a rival software vendor**](../playbooks/playbook-competitive-takeout.md): Pick a rival, understand where it's heading, find the accounts where its product is weakest, and open those accounts with a timely, evidence-based message.
