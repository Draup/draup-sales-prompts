# Prompts for SDR / BDR

Find who to contact and why now, then write outreach that references real facts.

37 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [One-page account brief](../prompts/01-account-research/account-brief.md) | A one-page brief on size, money, priorities, recent deals and any competitor in the stack, ending with who to call and why now. |
| [10-minute pre-call snapshot](../prompts/01-account-research/precall-snapshot.md) | Five short bullets you can read in the lobby: who they are, what just happened, where hiring is rising, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. |
| [Private company funding](../prompts/01-account-research/private-funding.md) | How much a private company has raised, its last round and valuation, equity history, whether it is in growth mode, and who signs. |

## Buyers and stakeholders

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Map the buying committee](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/buying-committee.md) | A ranked list of the people likely to buy, influence or block your deal in one function or workload, with their role in the decision, an entry point and the sponsor to reach later. |
| [New leaders in the last 6 months](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/new-leaders-warm-paths.md) | Leaders who joined or were promoted in the last 6 months in your area, where they came from, and which ones arrive from your customers. |
| [Warm paths among current leaders](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/warm-paths-current-leaders.md) | Senior leaders at the account who used to work at your customers, partners or your own company, so you can ask for a warm intro. |
| [Executive profile and openers](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/executive-profile.md) | A one-screen profile of a named executive and two openers tied to the account's current priorities. |

## Tech stack and displacement

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [What they adopted this year](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/recent-adoptions.md) | The tools first detected at the account in the last 12 months, each labelled as competing with, complementing or unrelated to what you sell, with any competitor product called out first. |
| [Displacement target list](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/displacement-list.md) | A sized, revenue-ranked list of companies in your industry and country whose stack includes a competitor's products, confirmed in the same pass, with likely-inactive and misfiled names set aside. |

## Signals and timing

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [What's worth acting on now](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/whats-actionable.md) | Up to 5 real triggers from the last 90 days, each tied to a stated priority, a likely owner and a suggested action, with the noise stripped out. |
| [Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md) | For one account or up to 5 per pass: the freshest relevant signal, the priority it supports, and a short, sourced opener for your persona that ends in one low-friction ask. |
| [Are they hiring more than usual?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/hiring-spike.md) | A plain-English verdict on whether job postings are running above or below normal, which workloads drive it, and whether it's seasonal. |
| [Are they really investing in X?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/investing-in-x.md) | A Yes / Early / No verdict on whether hiring, headcount and stated priorities back up a technology theme, with proof postings and talking points. |
| [What their job ads say about their stack](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/practice-shift.md) | The software tools in the account's job ads, matched against its installed stack and sorted into New, Building, Core and Coasting, so you see where people and budget are moving before it hits the press. |
| [Who in my market just [did X]?](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/news-question-templates.md) | A clean, dated table of the companies in your industry and country that just did one thing you choose: M&A, layoffs, leadership changes, expansion or new centres, funding, or partnerships. |
| [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md) | One table of the week's real triggers across up to 10 accounts, each with a so-what and an action, plus a clean list of accounts with nothing new. |
| [Weekly industry signal digest](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vertical-digest.md) | A one-screen digest of the week's material moves in one industry, grouped by theme, with the accounts worth touching. |

## Prospecting and target lists

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Build and size an ICP list](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/icp-list.md) | The true size of your ideal-customer list, size bands, and the top 25 accounts with money, providers, hiring, who to call and a why-now for the best 5. |
| [Who's hiring for a tech or role](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/hiring-led-list.md) | The end employers posting the most jobs for a technology or buyer role, cleaned of job boards and staffing firms, with who to call and the industries driving demand. |
| [Look-alikes of a best customer](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/lookalikes.md) | Up to 20 companies that look like your best customer on industry, size, HQ and shared technology, each with the reason it matches and who to call. |
| [Sell into a customer's competitors](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/competitor-set.md) | Your customer's real competitors, ranked by Draup's Opportunity Index score, with what happened at each in the last 90 days, a reference angle and who to call. |
| [Fastest revenue growers in my ICP](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/revenue-growth-screen.md) | The fastest-growing companies by revenue in your segment, with M&A-driven jumps set aside and a shortlist growing both revenue and headcount. |
| [Growing and shrinking companies: scale vs cost-out buyers](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/growth-and-cost-out.md) | Who in a vertical is adding people fast (scale-up buyers) and who is shrinking (cost-out buyers), with merged or closed companies removed and who to call. |
| [Startups with real momentum](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/startups.md) | The startups in a vertical that Draup scores for momentum, with what they do, funding, hiring and who to contact. |
| [Biggest logos in my territory](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/territory-logos.md) | The largest employers in a vertical in one metro, by Draup-estimated local headcount, with global capability centres (GCCs) called out and who to call. |
| [Is this inbound account worth it?](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/inbound-qualification.md) | A fast pursue / nurture / disqualify verdict on an inbound or referred account, based on your must-haves, with urgency from recent events, a read on the requester and a reply plan. |

## Playbooks for you

- [**First meeting in 30 minutes**](../playbooks/playbook-first-meeting.md): Walk into a first meeting knowing the account, the people in the room and the questions that will earn a second meeting.
- [**Win accounts from a services competitor**](../playbooks/playbook-displacement-campaign.md): Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message.
- [**Weekly signal routine**](../playbooks/playbook-weekly-signals.md): Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.
- [**Territory and list building**](../playbooks/playbook-territory-list.md): Turn an ICP into a sized, prioritised and tiered account list your team can work this quarter.
- [**Enter a new industry**](../playbooks/playbook-new-industry.md): Learn a new industry fast, see who wins there, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view.
