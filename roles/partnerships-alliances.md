# Prompts for Partnerships & Alliances

Find co-sell partners already embedded in your target accounts.

23 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Account research

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create](../prompts/01-account-research/acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. |
| [Private company funding](../prompts/01-account-research/private-funding.md) | How much a private company has raised, its last round and valuation, equity history, whether it is in growth mode, and who signs. |

## Buyers and stakeholders

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Warm paths among current leaders](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/warm-paths-current-leaders.md) | Senior leaders at the account who used to work at your customers, partners or your own company, so you can ask for a warm intro. |

## Tech stack and displacement

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Their stack in one category](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/stack-category.md) | What they run in one category, ranked by how actively each tool is used, with stale tools set aside, unfamiliar products explained and an optional vendor ranking with a consolidation read. |
| [Size a vendor's install base](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/vendor-install-base.md) | What a vendor sells and how many companies use each product, so you can size an attach, co-sell or competitive-replacement motion. |
| [Who's already working each priority](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/priority-stack-incumbent-map.md) | For each stated priority in one function: the tools linked to it, the service providers working it, and whether to displace, co-sell or go greenfield, judged for a software or a services seller. |

## Signals and timing

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Who just picked a vendor](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vendor-selection-news.md) | A dated list of companies in your industry that just selected, deployed or replaced a vendor in your category: competitor wins to watch, peer proof, and the implementation partners involved. |

## Prospecting and target lists

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Startups with real momentum](../prompts/06-prospecting-and-lists/startups.md) | The startups in a vertical that Draup scores for momentum, with what they do, funding, hiring and who to contact. |

## Outsourcing and services deals

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Outsourcing footprint and incumbents](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/outsourcing-footprint.md) | How much the account outsources, to whom, how concentrated its provider base is, and which workloads are ripe for vendor consolidation. |
| [A competitor's footprint at an account](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-at-account.md) | How deep a rival services firm is at the account, how it delivers, and the workloads where it is weakest: your wedge. |
| [Map my offering to buyers](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/offering-to-buyers.md) | Your offering mapped to Draup workloads you approve, then the 20 companies in your territory that outsource them most, with their incumbents and where the provider mix is fragmented. |
| [Profile a services firm (rival or partner)](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-profile.md) | A services firm's size, top customers, workloads and delivery hubs in your segment, ending in how to out-position it (rival) or where to team with it (partner). |
| [Who to partner with vs replace, by priority](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/partner-map.md) | For each priority in one function, the providers and tech vendors linked to it, and whether to partner, displace or walk into an open door. |
| [Global capability centres (GCCs) and the services play](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/gcc-opportunity.md) | The account's global capability centres (company-owned offshore or captive sites): where, which functions, how fast they hire, likely new sites, and whether build-operate-transfer (BOT), centre-of-excellence (CoE) expansion or managed services fits. |
| [Vendor mix vs the vertical](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/vendor-mix-benchmark.md) | Where the account outsources more or less than its industry, and which leading industry providers it doesn't use yet. |
| [Pick a co-sell partner](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/cosell-partner.md) | A shortlist of service partners already delivering your workload at the account and experienced with your technology, with the one to call first. |

## Industry and market intelligence

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Vertical vendor league table](../prompts/14-industry-intelligence/vendor-league.md) | Who really wins outsourcing work in a vertical, after removing agencies and staffing firms, plus top workloads and buyers and where your firm ranks. |
| [Contract vs full-time demand](../prompts/14-industry-intelligence/contract-vs-fulltime.md) | What share of hiring for a skill is contract versus full-time, and how much staffing firms drive it: a read on staff-aug and SI demand. |
| [Compare countries for delivery](../prompts/14-industry-intelligence/country-compare.md) | A side-by-side country comparison on labour force, unemployment, cost of living, inflation, tax and stability, with takeaways and questions for the client. |
