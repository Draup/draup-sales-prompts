# Prompts for Partnerships & Alliances

Find co-sell partners already embedded in your target accounts.

39 prompts, grouped by sales job.

**First:** [connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md), then run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) once per chat so every answer is tailored to what you sell.

## Start with these 5

| # | Prompt | What you get | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Which providers to partner with or replace](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/partner-map.md) | For each priority in one function, the providers and tech vendors linked to it with their engagements in that function, and whether to partner, displace or walk through an open door. | 2–4 min |
| 2 | [Pick the SI to co-sell with](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/cosell-partner.md) | A shortlist of service partners already delivering your workload at the account — counted within that workload, not account-wide — and experienced with your technology, with the one to call first. | 2–4 min |
| 3 | [Who's already working each priority](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/priority-stack-incumbent-map.md) | For each stated priority in one function: the tools and service providers already linked to it, rated where they're your competitors, and whether to displace, co-sell or go greenfield, judged for a software or a services seller. | 4–6 min |
| 4 | [Profile a services firm, rival or partner](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-profile.md) | A services firm's size, top customers, workloads and delivery hubs in your segment, ending in how to out-position it (rival) or where to team with it (partner), and a named person to act on. | 2–4 min |
| 5 | [Rival head-to-head in one industry](../prompts/09-competitive-intelligence/vertical-head-to-head.md) | Two services firms compared in one industry and country by active engagements, shared and exclusive customers and workload mix, with the shared accounts to attack, the workloads where you lead and a named person at the top target. | 2–4 min |

> **Before you trust a number**
> - Numbers: IT spend is a Draup estimate, often modelled from revenue, so use it for scale, not as a signal. Industry totals are global. Financials are in the company's reporting currency and fiscal year.
> - Freshness: news and hiring update continuously; leadership moves can lag by about three months, and brand-new executives may not appear yet.
> - People: some senior leaders have no function tag, so the prompts widen to C-level titles when needed. Draup can't search people by name.
> - Outsourcing engagements are Draup-derived records of work between a company and its providers, not published contracts.
> - Every drafted email ends with a "Check before sending" line listing any single-source or conflicting facts. Competitor tools are rated Red, Amber or Green by how entrenched they are.
> - Give the website the company actually uses (some parents use domains like home.barclays), and check names, titles and numbers before anything goes to a customer.
