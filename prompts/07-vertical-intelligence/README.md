# Industry and market intelligence

Industry-level numbers, priorities and vendor landscapes for pitches and planning.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**One-page vertical market snapshot**](vertical-snapshot.md) | A one-page view of an industry: revenue and spend, top priorities with likely owners, technologies gaining ground, and who outsources what to whom. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Industry point of view for a pitch**](industry-pov.md) | The 5 industry priorities that matter most for what you sell, each with dated company proof and a likely owner, turned into a 5-slide narrative and a one-line POV. | Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Vertical vendor league table**](vendor-league.md) | Who really wins outsourcing work in a vertical, after removing agencies and staffing firms, plus top workloads and buyers and where your firm ranks. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Where a vertical's teams sit**](talent-hubs.md) | The metros where an industry's IT and R&D teams are concentrated, with hubs that host both: where its delivery and engineering work happens. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 1–2 min |
| [**Top hiring companies in a vertical**](hiring-leaders.md) | The real end employers posting the most jobs in an industry, with outliers and recruiters removed, hiring intensity shown and who to call. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Contract vs full-time demand**](contract-vs-fulltime.md) | What share of hiring for a skill is contract versus full-time, and how much staffing firms drive it: a read on staff-aug and SI demand. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
| [**Compare countries for delivery**](country-compare.md) | A side-by-side country comparison on labour force, unemployment, cost of living, inflation, tax and stability, with takeaways and questions for the client. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
