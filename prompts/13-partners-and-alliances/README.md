# Partners and alliances

Pick, plan and grow co-sell motions with the partners already embedded in your accounts.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Which SIs to recruit in an industry**](recruit-si-shortlist.md) | A ranked shortlist of systems integrators that deliver your workload in one industry and already work with your technology, with anchor customers, delivery hubs, the one to recruit first and who to call there. | Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Accounts a partner can open for you**](sourced-pipeline.md) | A sized list of companies in your industry and country where a named partner already delivers work and your product (or a rival's) isn't in the stack, with how deep the partner is at the top 5 and a named buyer at the best one. | Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Joint account plan with a partner**](joint-account-plan.md) | A one-page plan you and a partner can both sign up to for one account: where the partner already works in your function, the priorities there, your technology's and a rival's position, who to meet and a 90-day plan with owners on both sides. | Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
| [**Is this partner rising or slowing?**](momentum-check.md) | A Rising, Steady or Slowing verdict on a prospective partner, scored from its hiring, headcount growth, client wins, acquisitions and footprint in your industry and country, with who to talk to there. | Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Attach targets on a partner platform**](platform-attach.md) | A sized, ranked list of companies in your industry and country that run a platform you integrate with and show no sign of your rival add-on, with the platform confirmed, a dated why-now and a named owner for the top 3. | Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Enablement brief for a partner's sellers**](enablement-brief.md) | A brief you hand to a partner's sales team: where your product fits their practice in your industry, which of their customers run your platform or a rival's, a joint value proposition, 5 qualification questions and 3 objection handlers. | Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
| [**Compare candidate partners head to head**](compare-candidates.md) | A weighted scorecard comparing 2–3 candidate partners in one industry and country on workload depth, technology experience, customer breadth, onshore delivery and momentum, with a pick, each candidate's main risk and who to call at the pick. | Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
