# Tech stack and displacement

See what they run, what's new, what's fading, and where a competitor is installed.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**What they run in one tech area**](stack-category.md) | What they run in one area, ranked by how central each tool is, with your competitors rated Red, Amber or Green, stale tools set aside and a named person to call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Is a competitor installed?**](competitor-installed.md) | A clear verdict on whether a competitor's products are live in the account's stack, rated Red, Amber or Green, framed correctly as a rival to displace, an adjacent tool or a platform you run on, with a named person to call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**What tools they adopted this year**](recent-adoptions.md) | The tools first detected at the account in the last 12 months, labelled as competing with, complementing or unrelated to what you sell, with any new competitor product flagged Amber first and a named person to call. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**Fading tools and replacement windows**](fading-stack.md) | The tools that have gone quiet in one area, what seems to be taking their place, and the 3 best-timed replacement windows, each with a dated reason and a named person to call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Size a vendor's install base**](vendor-install-base.md) | What a vendor sells and how many companies use each product, so you can size an attach, co-sell or competitive-replacement motion, with a named person at the vendor or a first target to approach. | Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM, Presales & Solutions | 1–2 min |
| [**Who's already working each priority**](priority-stack-incumbent-map.md) | For each stated priority in one function: the tools and service providers already linked to it, rated where they're your competitors, and whether to displace, co-sell or go greenfield, judged for a software or a services seller. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Target list: companies running a competitor**](displacement-list.md) | A true count and a ranked list of companies in your industry and country whose stack includes a competitor's products, with usage strength shown, distributors and inactive names flagged, and a named person at the top account. | SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
