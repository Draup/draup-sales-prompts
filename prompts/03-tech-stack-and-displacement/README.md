# Tech stack and displacement

See what they run, what's new, what's fading, and where a competitor is installed.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Their stack in one category**](stack-category.md) | What they run in one category, ranked by how actively each tool is used, with stale tools set aside, unfamiliar products explained and an optional vendor ranking with a consolidation read. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Is a competitor installed?**](competitor-installed.md) | A clear verdict on whether a rival's products are live anywhere in the account's stack, how central they are, who else is in the room and which stated priorities could justify a switch. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**What they adopted this year**](recent-adoptions.md) | The tools first detected at the account in the last 12 months, each labelled as competing with, complementing or unrelated to what you sell, with any competitor product called out first. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**Fading stack: replacement windows**](fading-stack.md) | The tools that have gone quiet in one area, what seems to be taking their place, and the 3 best-timed replacement pitches. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Size a vendor's install base**](vendor-install-base.md) | What a vendor sells and how many companies use each product, so you can size an attach, co-sell or competitive-replacement motion. | Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM, Presales & Solutions | 1–2 min |
| [**Who's already working each priority**](priority-stack-incumbent-map.md) | For each stated priority in one function: the tools linked to it, the service providers working it, and whether to displace, co-sell or go greenfield, judged for a software or a services seller. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Displacement target list**](displacement-list.md) | A sized, revenue-ranked list of companies in your industry and country whose stack includes a competitor's products, confirmed in the same pass, with likely-inactive and misfiled names set aside. | SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
