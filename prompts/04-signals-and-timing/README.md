# Signals and timing

Reach out when something just changed: hiring spikes, leadership moves, news, new tools.

11 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**What's worth acting on now**](whats-actionable.md) | An Act, Watch or Skip verdict and up to 5 real triggers from the last 90 days, kept or dropped with what you sell in mind (incidents and regulatory inquiries count if they bear on your offer), each with a named owner and a source count. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Signal-led emails for up to 10 accounts**](signal-to-email.md) | For one account or up to 10 per pass: the most relevant recent signal (preferring your territory), the priority it supports, a fact check, and a short opener written in your persona's language that ends in one easy ask. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Are they hiring more than usual?**](hiring-spike.md) | A plain-English verdict on whether job postings in the function you sell into are running above or below normal, which workloads drive it, whether it's seasonal, and who to call. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Are they really investing in X?**](investing-in-x.md) | A Yes / Early / No verdict on whether hiring, headcount and stated priorities back up a technology theme, with proof postings, three talking points and a named person to call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**Which tools their job ads are building**](practice-shift.md) | The software tools in the account's job ads, matched against its installed stack and sorted into New, Building, Core and Coasting, so you see where people and budget are moving before it hits the press, with a named person to call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, SDR / BDR, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Priorities with no hiring behind them**](priorities-vs-hiring.md) | Which stated priorities in your function have hiring behind them and which don't: the gaps where the account may need to buy rather than build, with a named person to call. | Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**Who in my market just [did X]?**](news-question-templates.md) | A clean, dated list of the companies in your industry and country that just did one thing you choose (M&A, layoffs, leadership changes, expansion or new centres, funding, or partnerships), with source counts and a named person at the best account. | SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Who just picked a vendor**](vendor-selection-news.md) | A dated list of companies in your industry that just selected, deployed or replaced a vendor in your category: competitor wins to watch, peer proof, and the implementation partners involved, with a named person at the best account. | Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Partnerships & Alliances, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**What changed since [Date]**](change-log.md) | A dated log of what changed at an account since a date you choose, ranked by impact on your deal, with competitor tools rated and a clear call: reopen, wait or watch for a lost deal; defend, expand or monitor for a customer. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Weekly trigger sweep for my book**](weekly-book-sweep.md) | One phone-sized table of the week's real triggers across up to 10 accounts, including any CEO or leadership change that takes effect in the window, plus the nearest item just outside the window for every quiet account. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 4–6 min |
| [**Weekly industry signal digest**](vertical-digest.md) | A one-screen digest of the week's material moves in one industry, grouped by theme, with source counts, the accounts worth touching and a named person at the best one. | Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR | 4–6 min |
