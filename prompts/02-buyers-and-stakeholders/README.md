# Buyers and stakeholders

Find the people who own the problem and the budget, and a warm way in.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Map the buying committee**](buying-committee.md) | A ranked list of the people likely to buy, influence or block your deal in one function or workload, with their role in the decision, an entry point and the sponsor to reach later. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**New leaders in the last 6 months**](new-leaders-warm-paths.md) | Leaders who joined or were promoted in the last 6 months in your area, where they came from, and which ones arrive from your customers. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Warm paths among current leaders**](warm-paths-current-leaders.md) | Senior leaders at the account who used to work at your customers, partners or your own company, so you can ask for a warm intro. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Executive profile and openers**](executive-profile.md) | A one-screen profile of a named executive and two openers tied to the account's current priorities. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Who uses [Technology] there**](personas-by-tool.md) | Which roles at the account use a given tool, grouped into three buyer personas with the other tools they use and the message for each. | Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**Hypothesis-led discovery questions**](discovery-questions.md) | An opening insight and eight discovery questions, each built on a dated fact about the account, with the hypothesis behind it and what a good answer tells you. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Pre-fill MEDDPICC from outside-in data**](meddpicc-prefill.md) | A MEDDPICC sheet pre-filled with what Draup can show (metrics, buyer candidates, pain, competition) and clear 'must discover' flags for the rest. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
