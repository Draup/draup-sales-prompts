# Buyers and stakeholders

Find the people who own the problem and the budget, and a warm way in.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Map the buying committee**](buying-committee.md) | Who to open with and which sponsor to reach later, by name, then a ranked phone-readable list of the people likely to buy, influence or block your deal in one function or workload. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**New leaders and warm intro paths**](new-leaders-warm-paths.md) | Leaders who joined or were promoted recently in your area, where they came from, and current leaders who used to work at your customers, so you know who to congratulate and who can introduce you. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Warm paths among current leaders**](warm-paths-current-leaders.md) | Senior leaders at the account who used to work at your customers, partners or your own company, who on your side to ask for the intro, and why now. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Executive profile and openers**](executive-profile.md) | A short profile of one senior person, found by name or just by title, with time in role, past companies, what they likely own, a competitor check and two openers. If Draup has no one with that title, you're told so up front and given the 3 closest people. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Who uses [Technology] there**](personas-by-tool.md) | Which roles at the account use a given tool, grouped into three buyer personas with the message for each, and the senior person to target first. | Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**Discovery questions built on their data**](discovery-questions.md) | An opening insight and eight discovery questions, each built on a dated fact about the account, with the hypothesis behind it and what a good answer tells you. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Pre-fill MEDDPICC from outside-in data**](meddpicc-prefill.md) | A MEDDPICC sheet (the deal-qualification checklist) pre-filled with what Draup can show — metrics, buyer candidates, pain, competition rated Red/Amber/Green — and clear 'must discover' flags for the rest. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
