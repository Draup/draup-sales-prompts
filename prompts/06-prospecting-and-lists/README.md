# Prospecting and target lists

Build, size and qualify target lists from firmographics, tech, hiring and growth.

10 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Build and size an ICP list**](icp-list.md) | The true size of your ideal-customer list, size bands, and the top 25 accounts with money, providers, hiring, who to call and a why-now for the best 5. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Who's hiring for a tech or role**](hiring-led-list.md) | The end employers posting the most jobs for a technology or buyer role, cleaned of job boards and staffing firms, with who to call and the industries driving demand. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Look-alikes of a best customer**](lookalikes.md) | Up to 20 companies that look like your best customer on industry, size, HQ and shared technology, each with the reason it matches and who to call. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Benchmark an account against peers**](peer-benchmark.md) | One table comparing an account with its closest peers on growth, hiring, workforce, footprint and recent news, plus where it leads and where to ask questions. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Sell into a customer's competitors**](competitor-set.md) | Your customer's real competitors, ranked by Draup's Opportunity Index score, with what happened at each in the last 90 days, a reference angle and who to call. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Fastest revenue growers in my ICP**](revenue-growth-screen.md) | The fastest-growing companies by revenue in your segment, with M&A-driven jumps set aside and a shortlist growing both revenue and headcount. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Growing and shrinking companies: scale vs cost-out buyers**](growth-and-cost-out.md) | Who in a vertical is adding people fast (scale-up buyers) and who is shrinking (cost-out buyers), with merged or closed companies removed and who to call. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Startups with real momentum**](startups.md) | The startups in a vertical that Draup scores for momentum, with what they do, funding, hiring and who to contact. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Biggest logos in my territory**](territory-logos.md) | The largest employers in a vertical in one metro, by Draup-estimated local headcount, with global capability centres (GCCs) called out and who to call. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
| [**Is this inbound account worth it?**](inbound-qualification.md) | A fast pursue / nurture / disqualify verdict on an inbound or referred account, based on your must-haves, with urgency from recent events, a read on the requester and a reply plan. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 1–2 min |
