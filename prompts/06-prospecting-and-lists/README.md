# Prospecting and target lists

Build, size and qualify target lists from firmographics, tech, hiring and growth.

10 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Size your ideal-customer list**](icp-list.md) | The true count of companies matching every filter, distributors set aside, and the 5 accounts to start with, each with who to call and a dated why-now. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Who's hiring for a tech or role**](hiring-led-list.md) | The end employers posting most for a technology or buyer role, cleaned of job boards and staffing firms, with hiring per 1,000 staff, who to call and a dated why-now. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Find look-alikes of your best customer**](lookalikes.md) | Up to 20 companies that look like your best customer on industry, size, HQ and shared technology, each with the reason it matches, plus who to call first and why now. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Benchmark an account against its peers**](peer-benchmark.md) | How an account compares with its closest peers on growth, hiring in what you sell, your competitors in the stack and recent news, with discovery questions and who to call. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Sell into your customer's competitors**](competitor-set.md) | Your customer's real competitors, ranked, with what happened at each in the last 90 days, a reference angle and a named person to call first. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Find the fastest revenue growers**](revenue-growth-screen.md) | The fastest-growing companies by revenue in your segment, with M&A-driven jumps set aside, a shortlist growing both revenue and headcount, and who to call first. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Spot scale-up and cost-out buyers**](growth-and-cost-out.md) | Who in a vertical is adding people fast (scale-up buyers) and who is shrinking (cost-out buyers), with merged or closed companies removed and a named person to call first. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Find startups with real momentum**](startups.md) | The startups in a vertical that Draup scores for momentum, with what they do, funding, hiring, why they'd buy and a named person to contact first. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Find the biggest logos in your city**](territory-logos.md) | The largest employers in a vertical in one metro, by Draup-estimated local headcount, with global capability centres (GCCs) called out and a named person to call first. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
| [**Is this inbound lead worth pursuing?**](inbound-qualification.md) | A fast Pursue now / Pursue / Nurture / Disqualify verdict on an inbound or referred account: your must-haves, urgency tied to what you sell, a bake-off check against your competitors, and a reply plan. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
