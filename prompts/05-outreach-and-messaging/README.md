# Outreach and messaging

Research-backed emails, call scripts, LinkedIn notes and briefing notes, each grounded in dated Draup facts.

13 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Email a newly appointed executive**](new-executive.md) | The newest senior appointments in your area at one account, checked against a second source, the stated priority that fits the new remit, and a 90-word first-90-days email plus a short follow-up. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Email after a funding or investment**](funding.md) | A dated, fact-checked read of the account's latest raise or announced investment, where the money is going by function, and a 100-word email plus a LinkedIn note that ties it to one priority. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Email after an acquisition**](acquisition.md) | The account's recent deals sorted by type and status and fact-checked, the integration work it has described in its own words, and a 100-word email to a named reader aimed at that integration problem. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Email during cost cuts or restructuring**](restructuring.md) | A private read of the account's cost pressure and restructuring news, a sensitivity rating, and a careful 90-word email built on the company's own stated savings goal, with a softer alternative and a named reader. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Email after a new site or centre**](new-site.md) | The account's newest centres, leases and expansions, fact-checked and matched against its location footprint and local hiring, plus a 100-word email and a LinkedIn note aimed at what the site is being staffed for. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Email on a hiring surge**](hiring-surge.md) | Which function is hiring above its own normal, the workloads and roles driving it, a named reader, and a 100-word email on the capability the surge is building, with no counting of their job ads in the copy. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Email on a new or rival tool**](new-technology.md) | Whether a technology or rival product is really in use at the account, how central it is (rated Red, Amber or Green if it's a competitor), where it shows up in public job ads, the priority it links to, and a 100-word email that never reveals stack tracking. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Cold-call opener, talk track, objections**](cold-call.md) | A 10-second opener, a 60-second talk track and three objection replies for one named person, each built on a fact-checked public fact or stated priority, plus three discovery questions. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**LinkedIn note and InMail to an executive**](linkedin-exec.md) | A confirmed profile for one named executive, a 300-character connection note with no pitch, a 110-word InMail tied to a stated priority in their remit, and a short message for after they accept. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Voicemail script plus follow-up email**](voicemail.md) | A 25-second voicemail for a named person built on one confirmed public fact, the matching same-day follow-up email, and a shorter second voicemail for day 4. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 1–2 min |
| [**Emails to three buyers at one account**](multithread.md) | A thread map for one account (economic buyer, technical buyer and champion), each named from Draup and tied to a different stated priority, with two fact-checked emails per person and a staggered send plan. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Re-engage a stalled or ghosted account**](reengage.md) | A dated, fact-checked list of what changed since your last contact, a reopen-or-wait call, a re-engagement email built on the change, a polite break-up email, and a version for a new person if one appeared. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**One-page brief before a C-level meeting**](exec-briefing.md) | A one-page, send-ahead briefing note for a C-level meeting (what you understand they're working on, what you'll bring, a 30-minute agenda and two questions), built only on confirmed facts, with a cover email and a private prep sheet of sensitivities. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
