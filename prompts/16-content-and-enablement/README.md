# Content and enablement

Turn Draup data into battlecards, ABM messages and email sequences.

5 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Battlecard: beat a rival services firm**](battlecard-services.md) | A one-page internal battlecard on a rival services firm: where it delivers in the work you sell, what it wins, recent moves, how to beat it, trap questions, the objection to expect and the first account to use the card on. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Battlecard: beat a rival software vendor**](battlecard-product.md) | A one-page internal battlecard on a rival software vendor: its most-used products, the largest end-user companies running them with usage strength, recent moves, how to beat it, the objection to expect and the first account to use the card on. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**ABM openers for 10 accounts at once**](abm-messages.md) | A table of short openers for up to 10 accounts per pass, each written to your persona in their language and tied to one stated priority and one dated signal from the last 60 days, graded Strong, OK or Weak, with sources and an optional joint partner note. | Marketing & ABM, SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 6–10 min |
| [**3-email sequence for one industry persona**](email-sequence.md) | A ready-to-load 3-email sequence for one persona in one industry, each email built on a priority in the persona's function and a dated move by a real company, plus the first account to load with a named contact. | Marketing & ABM, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**Explain where a Draup number comes from**](how-draup-knows.md) | A plain-English, shareable answer on how Draup builds a number (IT spend, outsourcing activity, talent flow and so on) and how far to trust it, written for the person who asked, with the help-centre link. | Presales & Solutions, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Marketing & ABM | 1–2 min |
