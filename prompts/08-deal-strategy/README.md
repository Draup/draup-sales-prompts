# Deal strategy and qualification

Win live opportunities: re-engage stalled deals, answer RFPs, build business cases, handle objections and beat a named competitor.

10 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Re-open a stalled deal**](stalled-reentry.md) | A reopen, wait or walk-away verdict on a stalled deal, tested against why it stalled, with the named person to call, the dated change that justifies it and a 90-word re-entry email. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**RFP research and bid/no-bid call**](rfp-research.md) | A bid, bid-to-learn or qualify-out call on an RFP, with the likely evaluation priorities, who is already there, the named panel member to reach, three win themes and the questions to submit in the Q&A window. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 6–10 min |
| [**Business-case inputs from their numbers**](business-case-inputs.md) | The size of the prize as a range, built on the account's own revenue, margin, spend, outsourcing and hiring, with every number labelled, a worked example you can edit and the named owner to validate it with. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Pick a realistic signature window**](budget-timing.md) | Whether your close date fits the buyer's fiscal calendar and budget posture, the events that move money in the next two quarters, the best 4–8 week signature window and the named person to confirm it with. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Answer objections with their own facts**](objection-handler.md) | Which objections are real and which are cover, with a short reply and a question back for each, built on dated facts about the account, and the named person to take the answer to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Win plan against a rival**](competitive-strategy.md) | How entrenched a named rival is at the account (rated Red, Amber or Green), the pitch they'll likely make, your wedge, three landmine questions and the named person to align with first. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Prepare for their procurement team**](procurement-landscape.md) | The procurement stance to expect (consolidate, cost-down, re-bid or strategic), who already supplies the work in scope and how concentrated they are, the pressure procurement is under and the named procurement lead to reach. | Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Arm your champion to sell internally**](champion-pack.md) | A forwardable brief your champion can use to sell the deal inside: why now in their company's words, cost of waiting, answers for the CFO, CIO and procurement, a 150-word internal email, and who to route it to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Reality-check a forecasted deal**](forecast-check.md) | An outside-in score of a forecasted deal on pain, money, timing, people and competition, a recommended forecast category, the questions to ask the rep and the named buyer to verify with. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager | 4–6 min |
| [**Mutual action plan on their calendar**](mutual-action-plan.md) | A mutual action plan built backwards from your close date that respects the buyer's fiscal calendar, names buyer owners at each step, flags events that could move dates and says who to send the draft to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
