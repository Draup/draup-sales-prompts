# Account research

Know the account before you call: size, money, priorities, filings and earnings calls.

12 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**One-page account brief**](account-brief.md) | Bottom line and a named person to call with a dated why-now, then the priorities in your function, the people who likely own them, any competitor in the stack and the key numbers. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**10-minute pre-call snapshot**](precall-snapshot.md) | Bottom line and who to contact first, then five lobby-length bullets: who they are, what just happened, where hiring in your function is moving, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions | 1–2 min |
| [**Is their budget growing or shrinking?**](budget-posture.md) | A verdict on whether the account's budget is growing, holding or shrinking, the deal size and pitch that fit, and who to take it to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Which priority to anchor your pitch on**](priorities-by-function.md) | The single priority to lead with, who likely owns it by name, where the account's priorities cluster and the vendors linked to each. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Their leaders' own words on [Topic]**](proof-points.md) | Up to six verbatim leadership quotes on your topic with speaker and fiscal quarter, who to take them to, or the annual-filing sections to read when there's no call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Numbers from the latest earnings call**](numbers-from-the-call.md) | The three numbers from the latest earnings call that matter for your deal, the question each opens and who to raise them with, plus every stated number in a reference table. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**What changed between two earnings calls**](call-over-call.md) | Themes that are new, stronger, weaker or dropped between the last two earnings calls, each with a quote, who owns them, and a 2-line talk track for your next QBR. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Which region should own this account**](buying-center.md) | Which region should own the pursuit, where to hold the first meeting and with whom, backed by revenue by region, top metros and where your function's leaders sit. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Business units worth targeting first**](business-units.md) | The two business units to target first, ranked by growth, with the priority behind each and a named leader to approach. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Openings from their recent acquisitions**](acquisitions.md) | The top 3 integration openings from the account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, who likely owns each, and an optional read of its strategic investments. | Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Private company funding and who signs**](private-funding.md) | How much a private company has raised, its last round and valuation, whether it is in growth mode, and who signs, by name. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps, Partnerships & Alliances | 1–2 min |
| [**Account SWOT, outside-in**](swot.md) | A SWOT of the account built only from dated Draup evidence, ending with where your offer fits and the named person to take it to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
