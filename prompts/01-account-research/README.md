# Account research

Know the account before you call: size, money, priorities, filings and earnings calls.

12 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**One-page account brief**](account-brief.md) | A one-page brief on size, money, priorities, recent deals and any competitor in the stack, ending with who to call and why now. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**10-minute pre-call snapshot**](precall-snapshot.md) | Five short bullets you can read in the lobby: who they are, what just happened, where hiring is rising, the priority closest to your offer, and one question to ask. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions | 1–2 min |
| [**Is their budget growing or shrinking?**](budget-posture.md) | A verdict on whether the account's budget is growing, holding or shrinking, the IT spend split, and the deal size and pitch that fit. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Which priority to anchor your pitch on**](priorities-by-function.md) | Where the account's priorities cluster, how each fits what you sell, and the single priority to lead with, with its likely owner and linked vendors. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Their leaders' own words on [Topic]**](proof-points.md) | Up to six verbatim quotes from leadership on your topic, with speaker, fiscal quarter and call date, or the annual-filing sections to read when there's no call. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Account & Customer Success Manager | 2–4 min |
| [**Numbers from the latest call**](numbers-from-the-call.md) | Every number management stated on the latest earnings call (guidance, targets, KPIs, investment plans) in one table, with the three that matter for your deal and the question each opens. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**What changed between two calls**](call-over-call.md) | Themes that are new, stronger, weaker or dropped between the last two earnings calls, each with a quote, plus a 2-line talk track for your next QBR. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Which region should own this account**](buying-center.md) | Which region and metros hold the revenue, people and decision makers for your function, which region should own the pursuit, and where to hold the first meeting. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Fastest-growing business units**](business-units.md) | Business units ranked by revenue growth, with the two worth targeting, the priority behind each and the unit leader's title. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Acquisitions, strategic bets and the openings they create**](acquisitions.md) | The account's acquisitions in the last 3 years, grouped by theme, the integration work each creates and the top 3 openings for you, plus an optional read of its strategic investments. | Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions, Account & Customer Success Manager, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Private company funding**](private-funding.md) | How much a private company has raised, its last round and valuation, equity history, whether it is in growth mode, and who signs. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps, Partnerships & Alliances | 1–2 min |
| [**Account SWOT, outside-in**](swot.md) | A SWOT of the account built only from dated Draup evidence, ending with where your offer fits and who to take it to. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
