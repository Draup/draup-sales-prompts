# Outsourcing and services deals

For IT services, consulting and staffing sellers: incumbents, share of wallet, GCCs and wedges.

11 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Outsourcing footprint and consolidation wedge**](outsourcing-footprint.md) | How much the account outsources, to whom, how concentrated it is, and the workloads where a consolidation pitch for what you sell fits, ending with a named buyer and a dated why-now. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**A rival's footprint and your wedge**](competitor-at-account.md) | How deep a rival services firm is at the account (or, if it's absent, who really is), how it delivers, and the workloads where it's weakest: your wedge, with a named buyer and a dated why-now. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**A rival's customers you can win**](competitor-customers.md) | A ranked list of a rival's customers in your industry and country, how entrenched it is at each, and the 3 to attack first, with a named buyer at the best one. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Peers' outsourcing deals for social proof**](peer-deals.md) | Recent outsourcing engagements at the prospect's peers in the work you sell, ready-to-use proof lines, and who to send them to and why now. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Find the biggest buyers of my service**](offering-to-buyers.md) | Your offering mapped to Draup workloads you approve, then the 20 companies in your territory that outsource them most, their incumbents, where the provider mix is fragmented, and who to call first. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Profile a services firm, rival or partner**](competitor-profile.md) | A services firm's size, top customers, workloads and delivery hubs in your segment, ending in how to out-position it (rival) or where to team with it (partner), and a named person to act on. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Which providers to partner with or replace**](partner-map.md) | For each priority in one function, the providers and tech vendors linked to it with their engagements in that function, and whether to partner, displace or walk through an open door. | Partnerships & Alliances, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**Is outsourced work coming back in-house?**](reinsourcing-risk.md) | For each outsourced workload, whether the account is building the same skills in-house — re-insourcing risk, capability gap or cost takeout — plus fast-growing in-house teams outside its outsourced list. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Their capability centres and the services play**](gcc-opportunity.md) | The account's global capability centres (company-owned offshore sites): where, which functions, how fast they hire, likely new sites, a maturity read, the services play that fits, and who to call. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**How their vendor mix compares with peers**](vendor-mix-benchmark.md) | Where the account outsources more or less than its industry, which leading industry providers it doesn't use yet, and who to raise it with. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Pick the SI to co-sell with**](cosell-partner.md) | A shortlist of service partners already delivering your workload at the account — counted within that workload, not account-wide — and experienced with your technology, with the one to call first. | Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
