# Expansion and cross-sell

Grow existing customers: global account maps, whitespace, cross-sell, new budget holders and sponsor coverage.

8 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Map a customer's units, sites and leaders**](account-map.md) | A one-page map of a customer: business units and regions with growth, key sites, the units and brands its leaders sit in and recent acquisitions, marked Ours, Adjacent or Open, with the top white space and who leads it. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Find uncovered functions worth selling to**](function-whitespace.md) | Every business function at an existing customer scored on priorities, hiring and outsourced work against where you already sell, with the top 3 uncovered functions, the named leaders who own them and a bridge from your current footprint. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Cross-sell from their adjacent stack**](adjacent-tech.md) | The tools a customer runs next to what you already sell them, graded Active, Thin or Fading and rated for incumbent strength, with the priorities and job ads that justify a cross-sell, up to 3 plays and the named owner to pitch. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
| [**New budget holders outside our footprint**](new-budget-holders.md) | Senior leaders new in role (about 18 months or less) in the functions you don't yet sell to, plus announced leadership changes, each with the priority they likely own, a first-90-days angle and the one to contact first. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Subsidiaries and acquisitions to expand into**](subsidiaries.md) | A customer's acquired companies and subsidiaries, each checked for whether it still runs on its own (own hiring, size, people moving to the parent), ranked as expansion targets, with the named person to approach at the top one. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Land at one site, expand to others**](site-to-site.md) | From the site or capability centre you already serve, the sister sites doing the same work, ranked by workload overlap and growth, with each one's local leader and the next site to approach. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Check sponsor risk and relationship gaps**](sponsor-coverage.md) | A risk read on your executive sponsor (tenure, new bosses, announced changes), a coverage grid of who you know versus who matters, likely successors and the 3 named relationships to build next. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Expansion business case from growth units**](business-case.md) | A short, evidence-led case for expanding into a customer's fastest-growing unit or region: where growth is, the priorities funding it, a Draup-estimated spend pool, a 150-word executive summary and the named owner to send it to. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
