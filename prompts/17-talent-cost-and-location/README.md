# Talent cost and location

Cost-arbitrage, location and hiring-difficulty cases for services pursuits. Uses the Draup for Talent connector.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Offshore saving case from market pay**](offshoring-cost-case.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | A per-FTE and team-level base-pay gap for moving a role offshore, with the talent supply and hiring difficulty to show the team can be staffed. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Best city for a delivery team**](delivery-location.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | A ranked shortlist of metros in one country for a delivery team, scored on talent supply, cost, hiring difficulty and location risk with editable weights, plus the client's existing footprint. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Roles the client can't hire: staffing pitch**](hard-to-hire.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | Which of an account's roles are hardest to hire in its key location, with supply, demand and pay, turned into a staffing or managed-services pitch. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, SDR / BDR | 4–6 min |
| [**Do layoffs open an outsourcing deal?**](layoff-pool.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | Where and in which roles a company has cut jobs in the last 12 months, read as a possible outsourcing trigger, plus which skills are now more available in the market. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 2–4 min |
| [**Size a skill pool and its employers**](role-sizing.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | How many people hold a role in up to 3 countries, how fast supply is growing, and which companies employ the most, split into prospects and services competitors. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Is our rate defensible against market pay?**](pay-benchmark.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | Market pay for a role by employer, including named competitors, the markup a proposed or quoted rate implies, and the rate you'd need at your target markup. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Presales & Solutions, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Employer-brand gaps for an RPO pitch**](employer-brand.md) *(Preview: Talent connector)* | How a company scores as an employer against its hiring rivals, its three weakest areas from employee reviews, and RPO or employer-branding angles. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
