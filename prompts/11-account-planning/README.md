# Account planning and expansion

Account plans, entry points, risk checks, QBR prep and territory tiering.

11 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Draft an account plan**](account-plan.md) | An account plan led by the bottom line, the first play and who to call: buying committee, ranked expansion plays, incumbents (including installed competitors), risks and a 90-day plan. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 6–10 min |
| [**Best entry points: expand or new**](entry-points.md) | The five best ways in for what you sell, each marked Expand (we already serve it) or New, with dated evidence, incumbents, any installed competitor and the likely owner. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 2–4 min |
| [**Where they're building teams**](expansion-footprint.md) | The metros where the account is adding people in one function, with capability centres and likely new sites called out, net hiring over a stated window, and who to call. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Renewal risk check (red, amber, green)**](risk-check.md) | A Red/Amber/Green risk table for one customer — your named sponsor, senior exits, layoffs, growth, installed and new competitor products in your workloads, negative news — with an overall rating and two actions. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Who they lose talent to**](talent-competitors.md) | The companies an account loses people to and hires from, with net flow: the employers it competes with for skills, credible benchmark peers, and who to raise it with. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 1–2 min |
| [**Customer alumni now at your targets**](follow-champions.md) | Senior people who recently left one of your customers, where they went, and which new employers are on your target list, with any champions you name flagged first. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 2–4 min |
| [**QBR prep: talking points and asks**](qbr-prep.md) | Three talking points and three asks for your business review, built on what changed at the customer in your sponsor's function, set against the outcomes you delivered. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Tier my accounts by fit and timing**](propensity-tiering.md) | Up to 25 accounts scored 0–100 on fit to what you sell (hiring in your workloads, installed competitors) and timing (news, hiring, growth), tiered A, B or C with a spread you can act on, and the driver behind each score. | Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | 4–6 min |
| [**Carve balanced territories by geography**](territories.md) | An industry-and-country account list, counted first, cleaned of holding and private-equity firms, and split into territories by HQ region, then balanced to within 10% on Draup-estimated IT spend, revenue or count. | Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Brief for a C-level pursuit meeting**](cxo-pursuit-brief.md) | A one-page pursuit brief for a C-level meeting: the 3 workloads to lead with, the buyers, incumbents, a Go or Not-yet evidence check, what to learn in meeting 1 and a 90-day plan. | Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
| [**AI readiness against close peers**](ai-readiness.md) | A read on the account's AI hiring, priorities, new tools and outsourced AI work, its AI hiring compared with up to 4 close peers, and who to raise it with. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
