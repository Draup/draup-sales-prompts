# Sales leadership and RevOps

Size territories, segment books, plan pipeline and enable reps on new verticals.

8 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Which region gets the next rep**](territory-potential.md) | A ranked comparison of 2–5 regions for one industry — how many sizeable accounts each holds, how many are active in the work you sell, where your rivals already sit, and the revenue at stake — with the region to staff next and the first account to open there. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Which cities to base reps and events**](metro-heatmap.md) | A ranked map of the metros in one country where an industry's people, big local employers and the employers active in your workloads concentrate, telling you where to base reps, run events, or assign a named-account owner instead. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Sort my book into coverage tiers**](book-segmentation.md) | Up to 20 accounts sorted into Strategic, Growth, Maintain and Deprioritise on an editable potential-versus-momentum rubric that scores your own workloads and rivals, with the coverage model for each tier and the flags behind every placement. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Board slides: where we hunt next quarter**](where-to-hunt-slide.md) | Three board-ready slides for one industry — the market in numbers, the 8 accounts to hunt next quarter chosen on fit with what you sell, and what the team needs — with speaker notes and every number dated and sourced. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 4–6 min |
| [**Next quarter's target accounts, ranked by triggers**](pipeline-targets.md) | A territory-wide list of accounts ranked by distinct triggers in the last 90 days, activity in the work you sell and rival presence, with one dated why-now each, the person to call at the top account, and the off-list companies acting in your territory. | Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 6–10 min |
| [**Brief reps on a new industry**](vertical-enablement.md) | A rep-ready brief on an industry you're entering: the words to use, what it funds in your function, who buys and how the function is organised at a lead account, where your rivals already sit, the services firms you'll meet and 5 discovery questions. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions | 4–6 min |
| [**Rewrite our ICP from best customers**](icp-refinement.md) | The traits your 5–10 best customers share in Draup — industry, HQ, size, providers, activity in the work you sell — turned into a tested look-alike definition with the number of new companies it returns, the trade-off of each loosening, and the first look-alike to approach. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Which accounts each rep works this week**](coverage-check.md) | Your pasted accounts and owners checked against real triggers in the window, tied to what you sell, grouped by owner, with the 5 actions for this week, the named person behind the top action, and which reps' books have gone quiet. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Account & Customer Success Manager | 4–6 min |
