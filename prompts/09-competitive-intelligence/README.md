# Competitive intelligence

Track what rivals are winning, building and losing, and where you can take share.

8 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**What a rival did this quarter**](recent-moves.md) | The few moves a competitor made in the last 90 days that change your deals (customer wins, partnerships, launches, deals and leadership), the accounts to defend or attack, and the named person to call at the top one. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**What a rival is building next**](hiring-signals.md) | What a competitor is likely building or selling next, read from its own job ads (rising workloads, skills and new roles in the last 5 months), with proof postings, a talk track and the named person to brief. | Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive | 4–6 min |
| [**Where a rival's people are going**](talent-drain.md) | Who a competitor loses people to and hires from, its workforce trend, the clients absorbing its staff (a sign of insourcing or delivery strain) and the named person to talk to at the most exposed client. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Partnerships & Alliances | 2–4 min |
| [**Rival head-to-head in one industry**](vertical-head-to-head.md) | Two services firms compared in one industry and country by active engagements, shared and exclusive customers and workload mix, with the shared accounts to attack, the workloads where you lead and a named person at the top target. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**How a rival's strategy is shifting**](strategy-shift.md) | How a competitor's stated priorities moved across its last three earnings calls, the acquisitions, leaders and cuts behind each shift, what changes in your deals and who to brief. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances | 4–6 min |
| [**Where a rival makes its money**](revenue-mix.md) | A competitor's revenue by business unit and region with growth, plus what it sells most, read as where it will defend price and where it is exposed, with the dated move and person to act on. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM, Presales & Solutions | 2–4 min |
| [**Accounts where a rival is fading**](fading-installs.md) | Your target accounts rated by how strong a rival's installed products are (Entrenched, Present, Thin or Fading), the successor already in place, and the best 3 takeout targets with a named person at the top one. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 4–6 min |
| [**Quarterly competitor watch for the team**](quarterly-watch.md) | A one-page quarterly report on up to 3 rivals: a side-by-side scorecard including hiring in your space, each one's top moves and leadership changes, three team actions and a named account contact for the biggest threat. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Presales & Solutions, Partnerships & Alliances | 6–10 min |
