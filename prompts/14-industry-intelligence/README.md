# Industry and market intelligence

Industry-level numbers, priorities and vendor landscapes for pitches and planning.

7 prompts. Open one for its detailed prompt, quick version and follow-ups.

| Prompt | What you get | Best for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**One-page vertical market snapshot**](vertical-snapshot.md) | A one-page view of an industry, optionally scoped to your region: size and growth, the priorities that fit what you sell with dated company proof, the top buyers, and who to call first. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 4–6 min |
| [**Build an industry point of view**](industry-pov.md) | The 5 industry priorities that matter most for what you sell, each with dated company proof and a likely owner, turned into a 5-slide narrative, a one-line POV and a named first contact. | Marketing & ABM, Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Who wins services work in a vertical**](vendor-league.md) | Who really wins outsourcing work in a vertical, after removing agencies and staffing firms, plus top workloads and buyers, where your firm ranks, and who to call first. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | 2–4 min |
| [**Find where a vertical's teams sit**](talent-hubs.md) | The metros where an industry's IT and R&D teams are concentrated, hubs that host both, where to focus, and a named person at the biggest local employer. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 1–2 min |
| [**Find who hires hardest in a vertical**](hiring-leaders.md) | The real end employers hiring hardest in an industry, ranked on postings per 1,000 staff with recruiters and outliers removed, plus hot spots and a named person to call first. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | 2–4 min |
| [**Check if a skill market buys contractors**](contract-vs-fulltime.md) | What share of hiring for a skill is contract versus full-time, how much staffing firms drive it, what it means for staff-aug or SI demand, and a named end-employer contact. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
| [**Compare countries for a delivery centre**](country-compare.md) | A side-by-side country comparison on labour force, unemployment, cost of living, inflation, tax and stability, with takeaways, client questions and, if you name the client, who to talk to. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps | 1–2 min |
