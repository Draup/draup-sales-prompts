# Renewal defence

> **Goal:** Spot what has changed at a customer ahead of renewal, rate the risk, rebuild relationships with new leaders, and walk into the review with proof of value.

**For:** Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive · **Time:** 40–50 min · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. What changed since [Date]

Lists everything material that changed at the customer since a date you choose (up to 12 months back).

**Look for:** New leaders, new tools, hiring shifts or restructuring in the areas you serve.

Open the full prompt: [What changed since [Date]](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/change-log.md)

Quick version:

```text
What changed at [Company] since [Date] that matters for [What I sell] in [Business function]? Leadership moves, new tools, hiring shifts, new priorities, news and new financials, in one dated table ranked by impact.
```

## Step 2. Renewal risk check (RAG)

Rates renewal and relationship risk Red/Amber/Green with dated evidence, including your sponsor.

**Look for:** Any Red row, and whether your sponsor is still in role. A newly detected competitor tool is Amber until you verify it.

Open the full prompt: [Renewal risk check (RAG)](../prompts/11-account-planning/risk-check.md)

Quick version:

```text
Renewal risk check on [Company] from Draup: Red/Amber/Green for our sponsor, senior exits in [Business function], layoffs, workforce and revenue growth, newly detected [Competitor] tools and negative news. Overall rating plus 2 actions.
```

## Step 3. New leaders in the last 6 months

Shows leaders who joined or were promoted in your area in the last 6 months and where they came from.

**Look for:** New decision-makers you haven't met yet. Plan an introduction before the renewal conversation starts.

Open the full prompt: [New leaders in the last 6 months](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/new-leaders-warm-paths.md)

Quick version:

```text
Who joined or was promoted at [Company] in the last 6 months in [Workload]? Date, name, full title, previous company. Flag anyone who came from [Our customers]. Drop exits and unrelated rows.
```

## Step 4. QBR prep

Combines the outcomes you delivered with what changed, into talking points and 3 asks.

**Look for:** Proof of value tied to their newest priority, and the asks that lock in the renewal (executive sponsor, reference, expansion).

Open the full prompt: [QBR prep](../prompts/11-account-planning/qbr-prep.md)

Quick version:

```text
Prep my QBR with [Company] for [Review period]: annual financials, new priorities, leadership changes, hiring trend and key news, set against [Outcomes we delivered]. End with 3 talking points and 3 asks.
```

## What you finish with

A dated change log, a RAG risk view with owners, a plan to meet new leaders, and a value-led review brief with 3 asks.
