# Existing customer: account plan → risk → QBR

> **Goal:** Build or refresh the plan for a key customer, turn it into concrete expansion plays, check it for risk, and walk into the QBR with a point of view and clear asks.

**For:** Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps · **Time:** 45–60 min · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Draft an account plan

Lays out priorities, buyers, installed tech, providers and an optional SWOT.

**Look for:** The 2–3 growth areas with the strongest evidence. Mark any section Draup couldn't fill as a discovery item for the QBR.

Open the full prompt: [Draft an account plan](../prompts/11-account-planning/account-plan.md)

Quick version:

```text
Draft an account plan for [Company] from Draup: snapshot, [Business function] priorities in their own words, buying committee, incumbents, ranked expansion plays for [What I sell], risks and a 90-day plan. Cite source and date per section.
```

## Step 2. Entry points and whitespace

Turns the plan into the five best ways in for what you sell, each with evidence, incumbents and the likely owner.

**Look for:** One or two entry points you can name as an expansion ask in the QBR.

Open the full prompt: [Entry points and whitespace](../prompts/11-account-planning/entry-points.md)

Quick version:

```text
Find the top 5 entry points at [Company] for [What I sell] from Draup's opportunities in [Business function or Workload]: initiative, dated evidence, incumbent tech and providers, and the executive who likely owns it.
```

## Step 3. Renewal risk check (RAG)

Rates renewal and relationship risk Red/Amber/Green with dated evidence, including your sponsor.

**Look for:** Any Red row, especially exits in your sponsor's function or layoffs. A newly detected competitor tool is Amber: verify it before treating it as a threat. Each Red or Amber row needs an owner before the QBR.

Open the full prompt: [Renewal risk check (RAG)](../prompts/11-account-planning/risk-check.md)

Quick version:

```text
Renewal risk check on [Company] from Draup: Red/Amber/Green for our sponsor, senior exits in [Business function], layoffs, workforce and revenue growth, newly detected [Competitor] tools and negative news. Overall rating plus 2 actions.
```

## Step 4. QBR prep

Combines the outcomes you delivered with what changed at the customer, and produces talking points and 3 asks.

**Look for:** Talking points that link their newest priority to value you've delivered, and the 3 asks (expansion, reference, executive sponsor).

Open the full prompt: [QBR prep](../prompts/11-account-planning/qbr-prep.md)

Quick version:

```text
Prep my QBR with [Company] for [Review period]: annual financials, new priorities, leadership changes, hiring trend and key news, set against [Outcomes we delivered]. End with 3 talking points and 3 asks.
```

## What you finish with

An account plan with expansion plays, a RAG risk view with owners, and a QBR brief with talking points and 3 asks.
