# Territory and list building

> **Goal:** Turn an ICP into a sized, prioritised and tiered account list your team can work this quarter.

**For:** Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM · **Time:** 40–50 min · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Build and size an ICP list

Builds and counts the list from industry, location, size and technology.

**Look for:** A list sized realistically for your team's capacity. Check companies are in the right primary industry and flag any that look inactive.

Open the full prompt: [Build and size an ICP list](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/icp-list.md)

Quick version:

```text
Count every company with primary vertical [Vertical], HQ in [Country], [Minimum employees]+ staff (running [Technology] if given). Show the top 25 by revenue with providers, 90-day postings and who to call, plus a why-now for the best 5.
```

## Step 2. Fastest revenue growers in my ICP

Finds the fastest revenue growers in the segment, with acquisition-driven jumps flagged.

**Look for:** Organic growers to move up the list; growth that comes from an acquisition needs a different story.

Open the full prompt: [Fastest revenue growers in my ICP](../prompts/05-prospecting-and-lists/revenue-growth-screen.md)

Quick version:

```text
Show the 25 fastest-growing [Vertical] companies by revenue, HQ in [Country], with $[Minimum revenue]M+ revenue. Set aside likely M&A jumps, add who to call, and shortlist those also growing headcount.
```

## Step 3. Tier my accounts by propensity to buy

Scores up to 25 accounts on an editable Fit + Timing rubric and splits them into tiers.

**Look for:** That the rubric weights match how you actually win. Adjust them, re-run, and agree the top tier with the team.

Open the full prompt: [Tier my accounts by propensity to buy](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/propensity-tiering.md)

Quick version:

```text
Tier these accounts for [What I sell]: [Accounts]. One Draup company lookup by website, score Fit 0–50 and Timing 0–50 with the editable rubric, tier A/B/C and give each a one-line reason.
```

## Step 4. ABM messages for a target list

Gives the top-tier accounts a personalised first touch, 5 accounts per pass.

**Look for:** Strong rows (signal linked to priority) go to SDRs this week; Weak rows go to a nurture track.

Open the full prompt: [ABM messages for a target list](../prompts/09-content-and-enablement/abm-messages.md)

Quick version:

```text
For the first 5 of [Accounts], write a 2-sentence opener to a [Persona] tied to one [Business function] priority and one Draup signal from the last 60 days. Table with sources and dates, Strong first; then offer the next 5.
```

## What you finish with

A tiered territory list with the scoring shown, plus ready-to-send openers for the top-tier accounts.
