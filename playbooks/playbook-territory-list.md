# Territory and list building: ICP to territories

> **Goal:** Turn what your best customers have in common into a sized list, tier it on fit and timing for what you sell, carve it into balanced territories, and send the first touches to the top tier.

**For:** SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps · **Time:** 45–60 min · **Steps:** 5

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Rewrite our ICP from best customers

Finds the traits your best customers share, including activity in the work you sell, and tests a look-alike definition.

**Look for:** The tightest definition that still returns 15–100 new companies, and any customer that resolved to a subsidiary or venture arm.

Open the full prompt: [Rewrite our ICP from best customers](../prompts/15-sales-leadership-and-revops/icp-refinement.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first: find what [Best customers] share in Draup, including activity in my workloads, turn it into a look-alike definition for [What I sell], count the new companies it returns and name the first to approach.
```

## Step 2. Size your ideal-customer list

Builds and counts the list from that definition: industry, location, size and technology.

**Look for:** A count that applies every condition, sized for your team's capacity. Check companies are in the right primary industry, and flag distributors, holding companies and inactive rows.

Open the full prompt: [Size your ideal-customer list](../prompts/06-prospecting-and-lists/icp-list.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line first: how many [Vertical] companies HQ'd in [Country] with [Minimum employees]+ staff (running [Technology]) match every filter, distributors set aside. Then the 5 to start with, each with a dated why-now, and who to call first.
```

## Step 3. Tier my accounts by fit and timing

Scores up to 25 accounts on an editable fit-and-timing rubric that uses postings in your workloads, and splits them into tiers.

**Look for:** A real spread across A, B and C. If more than half land in one tier, it re-tiers by rank; agree the A tier with the team.

Open the full prompt: [Tier my accounts by fit and timing](../prompts/11-account-planning/propensity-tiering.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named person, dated why-now) first. Tier [Accounts] for [What I sell]: fit (postings in my workloads, installed [Competitor]) and timing (news, hiring, growth), A/B/C relative to the list, one-line reason each.
```

## Step 4. Carve balanced territories by geography

Carves the list into balanced territories, keeping whole regions together.

**Look for:** No territory more than about 10% off balance, and no city split across reps without a reason. If the universe is over 100 accounts, raise the size threshold first.

Open the full prompt: [Carve balanced territories by geography](../prompts/11-account-planning/territories.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first. Count [Vertical] companies (primary industry) headquartered in [Country] with [Minimum employees]+ staff, drop owned, holding and private-equity firms, and split the rest into [Number of territories] territories by HQ region, balanced within 10%.
```

## Step 5. ABM openers for 10 accounts at once

Gives the A-tier accounts a personalised first touch, up to 10 accounts per pass.

**Look for:** Strong rows (signal linked to priority) go to SDRs this week; Weak rows go to a nurture track.

Open the full prompt: [ABM openers for 10 accounts at once](../prompts/16-content-and-enablement/abm-messages.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first: for up to 10 of [Accounts], a 2-sentence opener to a [Persona], in their words, tied to one [Business function] priority and one 60-day signal. Strong first (signal ties to the priority).
```

## What you finish with

A refined ICP, a sized list, A/B/C tiers with the scoring shown, territories per rep, and ready-to-send openers for the top tier.
