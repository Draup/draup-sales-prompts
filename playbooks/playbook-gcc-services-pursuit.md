# Global capability centre (GCC) & outsourcing pursuit

> **Goal:** Size an account's outsourcing and captive-centre (GCC) footprint, read its sourcing intent, check the main incumbent, and build a pursuit brief for the executive sponsor.

**For:** Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions · **Time:** 55–70 min · **Steps:** 5

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Outsourcing footprint and incumbents

Shows who the account outsources to, for which workloads, and how concentrated it is.

**Look for:** Workloads with many small providers (a consolidation play) or one dominant incumbent (a displacement or second-vendor play). Ignore catch-all workloads.

Open the full prompt: [Outsourcing footprint and incumbents](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/outsourcing-footprint.md)

Quick version:

```text
Size [Company]'s outsourcing in Draup: providers, engagements, Draup-estimated outsourced headcount, top 15 IT and services providers by share, top 10 workloads, a concentrated-or-long-tail verdict and up to 3 consolidation workloads.
```

## Step 2. Global capability centres (GCCs) and the services play

Maps the account's company-owned offshore centres: where, how big, which functions and whether they're growing.

**Look for:** Whether the GCC footprint is expanding, flat or shrinking, and which functions sit there. That points to optimisation, centre-of-excellence expansion or a build-operate-transfer play. Treat site headcount as indicative.

Open the full prompt: [Global capability centres (GCCs) and the services play](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/gcc-opportunity.md)

Quick version:

```text
Map [Company]'s GCCs in Draup: metro, functions hiring, 12-month postings, indicative site size, likely new sites, IT and R&D footprint and peers' GCC counts. Give a maturity read and a services play.
```

## Step 3. Is work coming back in-house? (re-insourcing risk)

Checks whether the account is building in-house what it used to outsource.

**Look for:** Outsourced workloads where the account is now hiring heavily or opening sites. Position augmentation or transition support there, not pure outsourcing.

Open the full prompt: [Is work coming back in-house? (re-insourcing risk)](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/reinsourcing-risk.md)

Quick version:

```text
For [Company]'s top 10 outsourced workloads, compare in-house postings over the last 5 full months with the 5 before, check GCC sites and expansion news, and label each: re-insourcing risk, capability gap, cost takeout or no signal.
```

## Step 4. A competitor's footprint at an account

Shows how deep the #1 incumbent is and where it's thin.

**Look for:** The wedge workloads you'll lead with, and the incumbent's strongest areas to avoid.

Open the full prompt: [A competitor's footprint at an account](../prompts/10-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-at-account.md)

Quick version:

```text
How big is [Competitor] at [Company] in Draup? Engagements, relationship tier, workloads and delivery locations, its rank among the account's top providers, and 3 workloads where it is thinnest.
```

## Step 5. CXO pursuit brief

Pulls it together into a go/no-go brief for the executive pursuit.

**Look for:** The top 3 workloads to lead with, the named buyer for each, and a 90-day plan you can commit to.

Open the full prompt: [CXO pursuit brief](../prompts/11-account-planning/cxo-pursuit-brief.md)

Quick version:

```text
Build a CXO pursuit brief for [Company]'s [Business function] leader: money context, 3 workloads to lead with (priorities plus incumbents), senior buyers, an evidence check (Go / Not yet), what to learn in meeting 1, and a 90-day plan.
```

## What you finish with

A CXO pursuit brief: outsourcing and GCC picture, sourcing intent by workload, the incumbent's weak spots, the lead offer, buyers, a go/no-go call and a 90-day plan.
