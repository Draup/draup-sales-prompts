# Partner co-sell

> **Goal:** Find where partners are already embedded at a target account, pick the right one to co-sell with, and open jointly.

**For:** Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller · **Time:** 35–45 min · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Who to partner with vs replace, by priority

Shows which providers and vendors are already linked to each priority at the account, and whether each is a partner, a competitor or an opening.

**Look for:** Providers already working the priority next to yours. They have access and credibility.

Open the full prompt: [Who to partner with vs replace, by priority](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/partner-map.md)

Quick version:

```text
For [Company]'s top 8 priorities in [Business function], which service providers and tech vendors are linked to each in Draup? Tag each Partner, Displace or Neutral for [What I sell] and give a play per priority.
```

## Step 2. Pick a co-sell partner

Shortlists the partners already delivering your workload at the account and skilled in your technology, with the one to call first.

**Look for:** Your existing partners ranked first, then the one with the deepest footprint in your workload. That's your co-sell pick.

Open the full prompt: [Pick a co-sell partner](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/cosell-partner.md)

Quick version:

```text
Which service providers deliver [Workload] at [Company] in Draup, how entrenched are they, and which have [Technology] experience in its industry? Rank them, putting our current partners first, and pick the one to call first.
```

## Step 3. Profile a services firm (rival or partner)

Profiles the partner you picked: top customers, industries, workloads and delivery hubs.

**Look for:** Overlap with your target list and industry, and delivery locations near the account's sites. That's the evidence for your co-sell ask to the partner.

Open the full prompt: [Profile a services firm (rival or partner)](../prompts/06-outsourcing-and-services/competitor-profile.md)

Quick version:

```text
Profile [Firm] from Draup: customers and engagements, top 10 customers and workloads overall and in [Vertical or Country], top 10 delivery hubs with onshore/offshore split, then 3 out-position angles (rival) or 3 teaming angles (partner).
```

## Step 4. ABM messages for a target list

Writes joint first-touch messages for the shared accounts, using the Co-sell partner input, 5 accounts per pass.

**Look for:** Messages that pair the account's priority with a recent signal and read as coming from both firms, ready for both teams to send.

Open the full prompt: [ABM messages for a target list](../prompts/09-content-and-enablement/abm-messages.md)

Quick version:

```text
For the first 5 of [Accounts], write a 2-sentence opener to a [Persona] tied to one [Business function] priority and one Draup signal from the last 60 days. Table with sources and dates, Strong first; then offer the next 5.
```

## What you finish with

A co-sell pick with evidence (where the partner is embedded and how it overlaps with your list) and joint openers for the shared target accounts.
