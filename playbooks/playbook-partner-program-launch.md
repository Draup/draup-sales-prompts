# Launch a co-sell programme with a partner

> **Goal:** Choose the right services partner for an industry, check it's heading the right way, build the joint pipeline, plan the first account together and arm the partner's sellers.

**For:** Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM · **Time:** About 20 min with quick versions, 55–70 min with detailed · **Steps:** 6

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Which SIs to recruit in an industry

Finds the SIs that already deliver your workload in the industry and work with your technology.

**Look for:** Firms marked Recruit first (high in both lists and 3+ technology-related engagements), your current partners first. Single-account depth is flagged in the detailed version.

Open the full prompt: [Which SIs to recruit in an industry](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/recruit-si-shortlist.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named SI leader, dated why-now) first. Which providers lead [Workload] in [Vertical] in [Territory], and which have [Technology]-related work? Top 10; Recruit first needs both lists and 3+ [Technology] engagements.
```

## Step 2. Compare candidate partners head to head

Puts the 2–3 best candidates from step 1 side by side with weights you control. Reuse step 1's engagement counts if they're in this chat.

**Look for:** Whether the pick survives the sensitivity check, and each candidate's main risk (concentration, offshore-heavy, thin technology work).

Open the full prompt: [Compare candidate partners head to head](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/compare-candidates.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named leader at the pick, dated why-now) first. Compare [Candidates] in [Vertical], [Country]: engagements in [Workload] and mentioning [Technology], top-2 customer share, onshore share, headcount growth. Weighted scorecard, pick, sensitivity check, each one's risk.
```

## Step 3. Is this partner rising or slowing?

Checks the chosen partner is rising, not slowing.

**Look for:** A Rising or Steady verdict, and any signal scored 0: those are the questions for your first partner meeting.

Open the full prompt: [Is this partner rising or slowing?](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/momentum-check.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named partner leader, dated why-now) first. Is [Partner] rising, steady or slowing? Score its hiring trend, headcount growth, client wins and acquisitions in 12 months, and its footprint in [Vertical], [Country].
```

## Step 4. Accounts a partner can open for you

Lists the accounts in your segment where the partner already delivers work and your product isn't in the stack yet.

**Look for:** The band of matching accounts and, among the top 5, those with Deep partner depth in a workload next to yours: the ones the partner can credibly open.

Open the full prompt: [Accounts a partner can open for you](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/sourced-pipeline.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named buyer, dated why-now) first. Which [Vertical] companies in [Country] use [Partner] and show no [Not installed] products? Band the total, top 10 unflagged by revenue, the partner's depth at the top 5.
```

## Step 5. Joint account plan with a partner

Turns the best account from step 4 into a one-page joint plan.

**Look for:** Joint workloads with priorities linked to the partner, and a named owner on each side for days 1–30.

Open the full prompt: [Joint account plan with a partner](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/joint-account-plan.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step (named leader, dated why-now) first. Joint plan for [Company] with [Partner]: its engagements in [Business function] vs total, joint workloads and priorities, [Our technology] and [Competitor] in the stack, and a 90-day plan.
```

## Step 6. Enablement brief for a partner's sellers

Gives the partner's sellers a short brief in their own terms. Reuse step 4's account list as a cross-check for warm starts.

**Look for:** The first 3 customers it names, which run your platform with or without the rival: lead the enablement session with them.

Open the full prompt: [Enablement brief for a partner's sellers](../prompts/13-partners-and-alliances/enablement-brief.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and first 3 customers first. Enablement brief for [Partner]'s sellers in [Vertical]: their workloads and customers, who runs [Platform] (with or without [Competitor]), a joint pitch, 5 qualification questions, 3 objection handlers.
```

## What you finish with

A chosen partner with evidence behind the choice, a sized joint pipeline, a signed-off plan for the first account and an enablement brief the partner's sellers can use on their next call.
