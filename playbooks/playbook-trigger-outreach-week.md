# A week of trigger-based outreach

> **Goal:** Turn one week of real account triggers into timely, multi-channel outreach: every message tied to a dated public fact, routed to the right person, with a call and voicemail behind each email and a clean re-open for stalled deals.

**For:** SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager · **Time:** 60–90 min across the week · **Steps:** 6

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Weekly trigger sweep for my book

Monday: finds the week's real triggers across up to 10 accounts and tags each Act now, Plan or Watch.

**Look for:** The Act now rows and their trigger type: leadership change, funding or investment, acquisition, restructuring, new site or partnership. Note the accounts with nothing new; they feed Friday's step.

Open the full prompt: [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (named person, dated why-now). For [Accounts], list [Window]'s real triggers, including leadership changes effective in the window, each with a so-what for [What I sell]; give quiet accounts their nearest item outside it.
```

## Step 2. Email a newly appointed executive

Tuesday: writes the trigger email. Use this prompt for leadership changes; swap in the matching trigger prompt for funding, acquisitions, restructuring, new sites, hiring surges or new technology.

**Look for:** One dated public fact and one stated priority per email, and nothing in the copy that reveals how you found it. For restructuring triggers, check the sensitivity rating before sending.

Open the full prompt: [Email a newly appointed executive](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/new-executive.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (the named new leader, dated). Find the newest VP-and-above appointment in [Function] at [Company] in 6 months, confirm it with a second source, match a priority, and write a 90-word email and 50-word follow-up.
```

## Step 3. Emails to three buyers at one account

Wednesday: for the one or two accounts that matter most, opens three threads (economic buyer, technical buyer, champion), each on a different priority.

**Look for:** A named person for each role, or the title to find in 'Roles with no clear person'. Stagger the sends; never reuse a paragraph across threads.

Open the full prompt: [Emails to three buyers at one account](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/multithread.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (who to email first, named). Give [Company]'s economic buyer, technical buyer and champion each a different priority, name a person for each, and write two emails each with a staggered send plan.
```

## Step 4. Cold-call opener, talk track, objections

Thursday: call the people you emailed, with an opener and objection replies grounded in the same facts.

**Look for:** The reason to call should match the email they received, so the call feels like a continuation. Keep installed-tool findings in your prep notes, not in the script.

Open the full prompt: [Cold-call opener, talk track, objections](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/cold-call.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (who to call, named, and why today). From 2 confirmed public facts and [Company]'s priorities, write a 25-word opener, a 150-word talk track, three objection replies and three discovery questions for a [Persona].
```

## Step 5. Voicemail script plus follow-up email

Thursday, when they don't pick up: a 25-second voicemail and a same-day follow-up email that reinforce each other.

**Look for:** The same fact in the voicemail and the email, and a yes-or-no reply option in the email.

Open the full prompt: [Voicemail script plus follow-up email](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/voicemail.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (who to call, named, and why now). From one confirmed 60-day fact and one [Company] priority, write a 60-word voicemail, an 80-word follow-up email and a 40-word day-4 voicemail for a [Persona].
```

## Step 6. Re-engage a stalled or ghosted account

Friday: for stalled or ghosted accounts (including those with no new trigger this week), lists what changed since your last contact and writes a re-engagement and a break-up email.

**Look for:** The verdict: Reopen now, Reopen via a new person, or Wait. If a new leader appeared, send the new-person version as well.

Open the full prompt: [Re-engage a stalled or ghosted account](../prompts/05-outreach-and-messaging/reengage.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line first (reopen now, reopen via a new person, or wait) and the next step with a named person. List confirmed changes at [Company] since [Last contact date], then write a 90-word re-engagement email and a 60-word break-up email.
```

## What you finish with

A logged week of outreach: each Act now account touched by email, call and voicemail on a dated fact, the top accounts multi-threaded to three people, and every stalled deal either re-opened, handed to a new contact, or closed out politely with a date to revisit.
