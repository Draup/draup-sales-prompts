# Playbooks

Playbooks chain several prompts into a complete sales workflow. Run the steps in one chat so each answer builds on the last.

| Playbook | Goal | For | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Be ready for a first meeting**](playbook-first-meeting.md) | Walk into a first meeting knowing the account, the people in the room and the questions that will earn a second meeting. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | About 12 min with quick versions, 25–35 min with detailed |
| [**Take accounts from a rival services firm**](playbook-displacement-campaign.md) | Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM | About 15 min with quick versions, 45–60 min with detailed |
| [**Monday routine: who to contact this week**](playbook-weekly-signals.md) | Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR | About 15 min each week with quick versions, 30–40 min with detailed |
| [**Account manager: risk, QBR, expansion**](playbook-protect-grow-customer.md) | For an existing customer, see what changed, rate the renewal risk, rebuild any lost relationships, walk into the QBR with a point of view, and leave with a named expansion play. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | About 15 min with quick versions, 45–60 min with detailed |
| [**Outsourcing pursuit at a captive-centre account**](playbook-gcc-services-pursuit.md) | Size an account's outsourcing and captive-centre (GCC) footprint, read its sourcing intent, check the main incumbent, and build a pursuit brief for the executive sponsor. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions | About 15 min with quick versions, 55–70 min with detailed |
| [**Co-sell an account with the right partner**](playbook-partner-cosell.md) | Find where partners are already embedded at a target account, pick the right one to co-sell with, and open jointly. | Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | About 12 min with quick versions, 35–45 min with detailed |
| [**Territory and list building: ICP to territories**](playbook-territory-list.md) | Turn what your best customers have in common into a sized list, tier it on fit and timing for what you sell, carve it into balanced territories, and send the first touches to the top tier. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps | About 15 min with quick versions, 45–60 min with detailed |
| [**Break into a new industry**](playbook-new-industry.md) | Learn a new industry fast, brief the team on its language and buyers, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view. | Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | About 15 min with quick versions, 45–60 min with detailed |
| [**Build a quarter's pipeline for one solution**](playbook-solution-play.md) | Go from one solution area (for example cloud migration, data, ERP, security or HR tech) to a scored target list, qualified accounts with a named buyer, the incumbents to work around, and timed first touches that cite each account's own evidence. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Marketing & ABM | About 20 min with quick versions, 60–75 min with detailed |
| [**A week of trigger-based outreach**](playbook-trigger-outreach-week.md) | Turn one week of real account triggers into timely, multi-channel outreach: every message tied to a dated public fact, routed to the right person, with a call and voicemail behind each email and a clean re-open for stalled deals. | SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager | About 20 min of quick runs across the week, 60–90 min with detailed |
| [**Win a deal against a named rival**](playbook-win-competitive-deal.md) | When a named rival is in your deal, size how entrenched it is, line up the people and the arguments, arm your champion, and close on a plan the buyer has agreed. | Account Executive, Presales & Solutions, Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps | About 15 min with quick versions, 45–60 min with detailed |
| [**Revive a stalled deal or walk away**](playbook-rescue-stalled-deal.md) | Decide whether a quiet deal is worth reviving, find a credible reason to re-engage, rebuild the case with the buyer's own numbers and set a realistic new close date. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps | About 15 min with quick versions, 40–50 min with detailed |
| [**Take accounts from a rival software vendor**](playbook-competitive-takeout.md) | Pick a rival, understand where it's heading, find the accounts where its product is weakest, and open those accounts with a timely, evidence-based message. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | About 20 min with quick versions, 60–90 min with detailed |
| [**Map a strategic customer, size the expansion**](playbook-grow-strategic-account.md) | Map the whole customer, find the functions, units and sites you don't serve yet, secure the relationships that protect the account, and take a sized expansion case to your sponsor. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | About 25 min with quick versions, 75–100 min with detailed |
| [**Sales leader plan: market to coverage**](playbook-plan-next-quarter-territory.md) | As a sales leader, size the market for what you sell, pick where to add the next rep, tier the accounts, carve balanced territories, set coverage and next quarter's targets, then run the plan week to week. | Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | About 25 min with quick versions, 70–90 min with detailed |
| [**Launch a co-sell programme with a partner**](playbook-partner-program-launch.md) | Choose the right services partner for an industry, check it's heading the right way, build the joint pipeline, plan the first account together and arm the partner's sellers. | Partnerships & Alliances, Sales Leader & RevOps, Marketing & ABM | About 20 min with quick versions, 55–70 min with detailed |
| [**Run an ABM campaign for 10–20 accounts**](playbook-abm-campaign.md) | Pick 10–20 target accounts, tier them, write each one a personalised opener from its own priorities and signals, multithread the top accounts, nurture the rest and refresh the list every week. | Marketing & ABM, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | About 20 min with quick versions, 50–70 min with detailed |
