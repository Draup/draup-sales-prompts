# Playbooks

Playbooks chain several prompts into a complete sales workflow. Run the steps in one chat so each answer builds on the last.

| Playbook | Goal | For | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**First meeting in 30 minutes**](playbook-first-meeting.md) | Walk into a first meeting knowing the account, the people in the room and the questions that will earn a second meeting. | Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Presales & Solutions | 25–35 min |
| [**Win accounts from a services competitor**](playbook-displacement-campaign.md) | Find accounts where a rival services firm is the incumbent, pick the ones where it's weakest, and open with peer proof and a timely, evidence-based message. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Account Executive, SDR / BDR, Marketing & ABM | 45–60 min |
| [**Weekly signal routine**](playbook-weekly-signals.md) | Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches. | Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps | 20–30 min weekly |
| [**Existing customer: account plan → risk → QBR**](playbook-account-plan-qbr.md) | Build or refresh the plan for a key customer, turn it into concrete expansion plays, check it for risk, and walk into the QBR with a point of view and clear asks. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 45–60 min |
| [**Global capability centre (GCC) & outsourcing pursuit**](playbook-gcc-services-pursuit.md) | Size an account's outsourcing and captive-centre (GCC) footprint, read its sourcing intent, check the main incumbent, and build a pursuit brief for the executive sponsor. | Services, SI & Staffing Seller, Sales Leader & RevOps, Presales & Solutions | 55–70 min |
| [**Partner co-sell**](playbook-partner-cosell.md) | Find where partners are already embedded at a target account, pick the right one to co-sell with, and open jointly. | Partnerships & Alliances, Account Executive, Services, SI & Staffing Seller | 35–45 min |
| [**Territory and list building**](playbook-territory-list.md) | Turn an ICP into a sized, prioritised and tiered account list your team can work this quarter. | Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Account Executive, Marketing & ABM | 40–50 min |
| [**Enter a new industry**](playbook-new-industry.md) | Learn a new industry fast, see who wins there, build a first account list, and launch outbound with a point of view. | Marketing & ABM, Sales Leader & RevOps, SDR / BDR, Account Executive | 45–60 min |
| [**Expand an existing customer**](playbook-expand-customer.md) | Find where an existing customer is growing, pick the best way in for your next deal, map the people, and open with a timely message. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps | 40–50 min |
| [**Renewal defence**](playbook-renewal-defence.md) | Spot what has changed at a customer ahead of renewal, rate the risk, rebuild relationships with new leaders, and walk into the review with proof of value. | Account & Customer Success Manager, Sales Leader & RevOps, Account Executive | 40–50 min |
