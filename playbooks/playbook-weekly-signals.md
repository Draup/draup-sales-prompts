# Monday routine: who to contact this week

> **Goal:** Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.

**For:** Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR · **Time:** About 15 min each week with quick versions, 30–40 min with detailed · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Weekly trigger sweep for my book

Scans up to 10 named accounts for real triggers in the window and drops the noise.

**Look for:** Accounts with a leadership change, new site, deal, acquisition, funding or layoff dated inside the window, plus the nearest item outside it for quiet accounts. Outages and breaches count only if they bear on what you sell.

Open the full prompt: [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (named person, dated why-now). For [Accounts], list [Window]'s real triggers, including leadership changes effective in the window, each with a so-what for [What I sell]; give quiet accounts their nearest item outside it.
```

## Step 2. Weekly industry signal digest

Shows what moved across your industry, including companies not yet on your list.

**Look for:** Peer moves you can use as social proof, and the 3–5 accounts it says to touch; add any new name to your book.

Open the full prompt: [Weekly industry signal digest](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vertical-digest.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first (a named person, a dated why-now). This week's [Vertical] digest: the 10 most material moves with date, source count and why each matters for [What I sell], plus 3–5 accounts to touch.
```

## Step 3. What changed since [Date]

For the 2–3 hottest accounts from step 1, lists what changed since your last touch. Reuse the result from step 1 for news; this adds people, tools and hiring.

**Look for:** The verdict (customer: defend, expand or monitor; lost deal: reopen, wait or watch) and the named person behind it.

Open the full prompt: [What changed since [Date]](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/change-log.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line first (customer: defend, expand or monitor; lost deal: reopen, wait or watch) and next step with a named person. Then up to 10 dated changes at [Company] since [Date] that matter for [What I sell].
```

## Step 4. Signal-led emails for up to 10 accounts

Turns the week's best triggers into sourced openers. Reuse the triggers from steps 1–3: run it in the same chat so it doesn't search again.

**Look for:** One fact-checked signal per account in a 45-word opener; send today to the person named in step 1 or 3, and hold any account whose signal failed the fact check.

Open the full prompt: [Signal-led emails for up to 10 accounts](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line and next step first. For each of [Accounts] (up to 10), pick the 60-day signal most relevant to [What I sell] and my [Territory], fact-check it, and write a 45-word [Persona] opener ending in one ask.
```

## What you finish with

3–5 accounts to contact this week, each with the trigger, its date, the person, the angle and a ready-to-send opener.
