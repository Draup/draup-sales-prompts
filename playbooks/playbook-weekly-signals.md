# Weekly signal routine

> **Goal:** Spend about half an hour each Monday finding which accounts and industry moves deserve action this week, and send the first touches.

**For:** Account Executive, Account & Customer Success Manager, SDR / BDR, Sales Leader & RevOps · **Time:** 20–30 min weekly · **Steps:** 4

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Weekly trigger sweep for my book

Scans up to 10 named accounts for real triggers and drops the noise.

**Look for:** Accounts with a leadership change, new site, deal, acquisition, funding or layoff in the last 7–14 days. Ignore outages, stock chatter and look-alike names.

Open the full prompt: [Weekly trigger sweep for my book](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/weekly-book-sweep.md)

Quick version:

```text
For [Accounts] (up to 10), list executive changes, M&A, layoffs, partnerships, expansion and funding news from [Window], newest first, with source, a one-line so-what for [What I sell] and an action. List accounts with nothing material.
```

## Step 2. Weekly industry signal digest

Shows what moved across your industry, including companies not yet on your list.

**Look for:** Peer moves you can use as social proof, and any new company worth adding to your book.

Open the full prompt: [Weekly industry signal digest](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/vertical-digest.md)

Quick version:

```text
Give me this week's [Vertical] digest: the 10 most material M&A, leadership, partnership, expansion, layoff and funding moves, with date, company, source and why each matters for [What I sell]. Group by theme and name 3–5 accounts to touch.
```

## Step 3. What changed since [Date]

For the 2–3 hottest accounts, lists everything material that changed since your last touch.

**Look for:** What's different since you last spoke: new people, new tools, hiring shifts. That's your reason to call.

Open the full prompt: [What changed since [Date]](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/change-log.md)

Quick version:

```text
What changed at [Company] since [Date] that matters for [What I sell] in [Business function]? Leadership moves, new tools, hiring shifts, new priorities, news and new financials, in one dated table ranked by impact.
```

## Step 4. Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)

Turns the week's best triggers into sourced openers, so the routine ends in a sent touch, not a list.

**Look for:** Strong rows (signal linked to a priority) go out today; the rest get a call or wait for a better hook.

Open the full prompt: [Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md)

Quick version:

```text
For each of [Accounts] (up to 5), find one deals, expansion, executive or M&A signal from the last 60 days, pair it with a stated priority, and write a short [Persona] opener ending in one easy ask. Cite sources.
```

## What you finish with

3–5 accounts to contact this week, each with the trigger, its date, the angle and a ready-to-send opener.
