# Map a strategic customer, size the expansion

> **Goal:** Map the whole customer, find the functions, units and sites you don't serve yet, secure the relationships that protect the account, and take a sized expansion case to your sponsor.

**For:** Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps, Services, SI & Staffing Seller · **Time:** About 25 min with quick versions, 75–100 min with detailed · **Steps:** 7

**Step 0.** [Connect Draup for Sales](../guides/connect.md) and run [Tell your AI about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so your assistant knows what you sell. Then run the steps below in the same chat.

## Step 1. Map a customer's units, sites and leaders

Gives you the shape of the account: units, regions, top sites and recent acquisitions.

**Look for:** The 3 biggest open areas and the Ours / Adjacent / Open labels. Tell it where you are today or the map won't show white space.

Open the full prompt: [Map a customer's units, sites and leaders](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/account-map.md)

Quick version:

```text
Map [Company] on one page. Lead with the 3 biggest open areas for [What I sell] and a named leader to approach. Show units, regions and top sites with growth, marked Ours, Adjacent or Open, and recent acquisitions.
```

## Step 2. Find uncovered functions worth selling to

Scores every function on priorities, hiring and outsourced work against what you already sell.

**Look for:** The top uncovered function and the named leader to approach, with a dated why-now.

Open the full prompt: [Find uncovered functions worth selling to](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/function-whitespace.md)

Quick version:

```text
Score every business function at [Company] on priorities, 12-month hiring and outsourced work. Lead with the top 3 uncovered functions for [What I sell] and the named leader to approach, with a dated why-now.
```

## Step 3. New budget holders outside our footprint

Finds senior leaders new in role in the functions you don't cover.

**Look for:** The leader to contact first, and any announced changes that could freeze or reopen decisions.

Open the full prompt: [New budget holders outside our footprint](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/new-budget-holders.md)

Quick version:

```text
At [Company], find senior leaders in [Functions we don't cover] 18 months or less in title. Lead with the one to contact first and the dated why-now, then announced changes, linked priorities and first-90-days angles.
```

## Step 4. Land at one site, expand to others

Optional: run it when you've landed at one site or capability centre. Reuse the sites from step 1 instead of pulling them again.

**Look for:** The next site to approach and its named local leader your sponsor can introduce.

Open the full prompt: [Land at one site, expand to others](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/site-to-site.md)

Quick version:

```text
Starting from [Site we serve] at [Company], rank the sister sites for [Business function]. Lead with the next site to approach and its named local leader with a dated why-now. Compare size, postings and what each site is hiring for.
```

## Step 5. Check sponsor risk and relationship gaps

Checks how exposed you are to one sponsor. Reuse the leaders from steps 2–3 as the people to cover.

**Look for:** The sponsor's risk rating, empty economic-buyer cells and the next relationship to build. Keep this internal.

Open the full prompt: [Check sponsor risk and relationship gaps](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/sponsor-coverage.md)

Quick version:

```text
Rate [Our sponsor]'s risk at [Company] (tenure, new leaders above, announced changes). Lead with the risk and the named person to build a relationship with next, then a coverage grid and likely successors.
```

## Step 6. Expansion business case from growth units

Builds a sized case that rides the customer's own growth story.

**Look for:** Priorities in management's words, a spend range with the formula shown, and the 150-word summary your sponsor can forward.

Open the full prompt: [Expansion business case from growth units](../prompts/12-expansion-and-cross-sell/business-case.md)

Quick version:

```text
Build an expansion case for [Expansion idea] at [Company]. Lead with the size of the opportunity and the named owner to send it to. Show growth units, supporting priorities, a Draup-estimated spend pool and a 150-word summary.
```

## Step 7. Draft an account plan

Pulls everything into an account plan. Reuse the results from steps 1–6: run it in the same chat and tell it not to re-pull the committee or priorities.

**Look for:** The first play and named contact, and ranked plays that match the white space and new budget holders you found.

Open the full prompt: [Draft an account plan](../prompts/11-account-planning/account-plan.md)

Quick version:

```text
Bottom line, first play and a named contact with a dated why-now first. Then an account plan for [Company]: [Business function] priorities, buying committee, incumbents and installed [Competitor] products, ranked plays for [What I sell], risks, 90-day plan.
```

## What you finish with

An account growth plan: a one-page map with white space marked, the top expansion function and site, the new budget holders and relationship gaps to close, and a sized business case ready for your sponsor.
