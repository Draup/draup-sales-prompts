# Expand an existing customer

**Goal:** Find where an existing customer is growing, pick the best way in for your next deal, map the people, and open with a timely message.

**For:** Account & Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, Sales Leader & RevOps · **Time:** 40–50 min

**Step 0.** Run [Tell Claude about you](../prompts/00-start-here/about-me.md) so Claude knows what you sell.

## Step 1. Where they're building teams

Shows the metros where the customer is adding people in one function, with new sites called out.

**Look for:** Locations or functions growing fastest. New sites are inferred from postings, so confirm them in conversation.

Open the full prompt: [Where they're building teams](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/expansion-footprint.md)

Quick version:

```text
Where is [Company] building [Business function] teams? Top 10 metros by 12-month postings with growth index, HQ/GCC tags, indicative site size and net hiring (state the window), plus likely new sites.
```

## Step 2. Entry points and whitespace

Ranks the five best ways in for what you sell, each with evidence, incumbents and the likely owner.

**Look for:** The entry point with the strongest evidence and the fewest incumbents. That's your next deal.

Open the full prompt: [Entry points and whitespace](../prompts/08-account-planning-and-expansion/entry-points.md)

Quick version:

```text
Find the top 5 entry points at [Company] for [What I sell] from Draup's opportunities in [Business function or Workload]: initiative, dated evidence, incumbent tech and providers, and the executive who likely owns it.
```

## Step 3. Map the buying committee

Maps who buys, influences or could block in that function.

**Look for:** The entry point (likely champion), the sponsor to reach later (economic buyer) and any potential blocker.

Open the full prompt: [Map the buying committee](../prompts/02-buyers-and-stakeholders/buying-committee.md)

Quick version:

```text
Map the buying committee at [Company] for [Business function or Workload]: up to 12 people with full title, unit, location, budget control and likely role (economic buyer, technical buyer, champion, influencer, potential blocker), plus an entry point and a sponsor.
```

## Step 4. Who's already working each priority

Shows who's already working each priority, labelled co-sell or displace for what you sell.

**Look for:** A priority with no incumbent (greenfield) or one where you can co-sell with a provider already there.

Open the full prompt: [Who's already working each priority](../prompts/03-tech-stack-and-displacement/priority-stack-incumbent-map.md)

Quick version:

```text
Map [Company]'s [Business function] priorities to the tools and service providers linked to each. Check the stack for [Competitor] products, and tag each priority Displace, Co-sell, Greenfield or Not ours for a [software / services] seller.
```

## Step 5. Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)

Writes a sourced opener for the entry-point owner, tied to a recent signal.

**Look for:** A signal from the last 60 days that links to the entry point. If there's none, lead with the priority and a proof point.

Open the full prompt: [Signal-to-email (one or a few accounts)](../prompts/04-signals-and-timing/signal-to-email.md)

Quick version:

```text
For each of [Accounts] (up to 5), find one deals, expansion, executive or M&A signal from the last 60 days, pair it with a stated priority, and write a short [Persona] opener ending in one easy ask. Cite sources.
```

## What you finish with

One expansion play with the evidence, the people to reach in order, the incumbents to work with or around, and a ready-to-send opener.
